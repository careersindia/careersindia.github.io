ℹ️Full Form

Cost and Management Accountant - Final Course. This is the third and final level of the CMA course offered by ICMAI.

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: A candidate must have cleared both groups of the CMA Intermediate Examination.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for appearing in the CMA Final exam.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The course is divided into two groups with four papers each, testing expert-level strategic knowledge:

  • Group III: Corporate & Economic Laws, Strategic Financial Management, Direct Tax Laws and International Taxation, and Strategic Cost Management.
  • Group IV: Cost and Management Audit, Corporate Financial Reporting, Indirect Tax Laws and Practice, and one elective paper from a choice of three (e.g., Strategic Performance Management & Business Valuation).

⚙️Path to Becoming a CMA

Passing CMA Final is the last academic hurdle to becoming a qualified professional:

  1. Pass CMA Foundation (or be eligible for Direct Entry).
  2. Clear both groups of CMA Intermediate.
  3. Complete Practical Training (15 months).
  4. Clear both groups of CMA Final.
  5. Become an Associate Member of ICMAI to be designated as a 'Cost and Management Accountant' (CMA).

💰Starting Salary Potential (After Qualifying)

A qualified Cost and Management Accountant is a high-demand professional with excellent earning potential.

  • Average Campus Placement Salary: Approximately ₹8 Lakhs to ₹14 Lakhs per annum.
  • Highest Salary: Can range from ₹20 Lakhs to ₹25 Lakhs per annum or more for rank holders and those placed in top PSUs or MNCs.

🏢Starting Post & Department

Qualified CMAs are recruited for various strategic roles in the finance, accounts, and costing departments of companies, especially in the manufacturing and service sectors:

  • Cost Manager / Management Accountant
  • Financial Controller / Analyst
  • Internal Auditor
  • Finance Director / Chief Financial Officer (CFO) with experience
  • They can also start their **own independent practice** in cost audit and consultancy.

📋Job Profile of a CMA

A CMA is a strategic-level professional who goes beyond traditional accounting. The role involves cost management, performance evaluation, strategic decision-making, and advising the management on pricing, budgeting, and business strategy to maximize profitability and efficiency.

🎁Benefits of the CMA Qualification

  • A prestigious professional qualification specializing in cost management and business strategy.
  • High demand in manufacturing, service industries, and PSUs for cost control and efficiency.
  • Expertise in strategic decision-making, which opens doors to top management positions like CFO.
  • Global career opportunities, as the CMA qualification has recognition in various countries.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The CMA Final exam is conducted **twice a year**.
  • Exam Months: In June and December.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must first register for the Final course with ICMAI after clearing the Intermediate level. Subsequently, they must fill a separate examination form online for each attempt.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

The exam is conducted in an online, center-based mode.

Paper Type Max Marks
All 8 Papers Objective (MCQ) & Subjective 100 per paper

Scoring Pattern: To pass a group, a candidate must obtain a minimum of **40% marks in each paper** AND a minimum of **50% marks in the aggregate** of that group. There is **no negative marking** in the objective (MCQ) part.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The duration for each of the eight papers is 3 hours (180 minutes).

⏳Result Time

The results for the June and December exams are typically declared by the last week of **August** and the last week of **February**, respectively (within 2 months).

💸Fees

The fee structure involves a one-time course registration fee (around ₹25,000) and a separate examination fee for each attempt (around ₹1,400 for one group or ₹2,800 for both). These fees are subject to change by ICMAI.
