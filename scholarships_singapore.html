Singapore International Graduate Award (SINGA)

HostSingapore, at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), National University of Singapore (NUS), Singapore University of Technology and Design (SUTD), or Singapore Management University (SMU).
Rankings / Featured In
  • NUS: QS World University Rankings (2025): 8th
  • NTU: QS World University Rankings (2025): 15th
  • SUTD/SMU: Specialized universities, highly ranked in their respective fields.
Websitehttps://www.a-star.edu.sg
LevelDoctoral (PhD)
Eligibility / Features
  • Academic Requirements: Must demonstrate a passion for research and have excellent academic results. A Bachelor's degree with at least a second-class upper honours or equivalent is typically required.
  • Standardized Test Scores: GRE is strongly recommended as it can significantly enhance an application. English proficiency (IELTS/TOEFL) is required if English is not the applicant's first language.
  • Field of Study: Biomedical Sciences, Computing and Information Sciences, Engineering and Technology, and Physical Sciences.
DeadlineThere are two application cycles. For the August intake, the deadline is typically December 1 of the preceding year. For the January intake, the deadline is June 1 of the preceding year.
ValueA fully-funded scholarship for up to 4 years. It covers full tuition fees, a monthly stipend of S$2,200 (which increases to S$2,700 after passing the Qualifying Examination), a one-time airfare grant of up to S$1,500, and a one-time settling-in allowance of S$1,000.
ApplicationApply online through the SINGA application portal. No separate application to the universities is needed initially. You must indicate your choice of research supervisors.
Documents
  • Statement of Purpose (SOP): A statement of purpose is part of the online application.
  • Letters of Recommendation (LORs): Two academic reference reports are required. Referees submit these online.
  • Academic Transcripts: Required from all undergraduate and/or Master's studies.
  • Proof of English proficiency (TOEFL/IELTS scores): Required if applicable.
  • CV or Resume: Recommended to upload.
  • Passport copy: Required.
EstablishedNTU (1991), NUS (1905), SUTD (2009), SMU (2000).
Undergraduate NUS (1905), NTU (1991), SMU (2000), SUTD (2009). N/A
