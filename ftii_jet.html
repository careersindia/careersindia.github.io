DETAILS ABOUT FTII-JET EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Film and Television Institute of India (FTII) - Joint Entrance Test (JET).

✅Eligibility

  • For PG Diploma Courses (Film Wing): Bachelor's Degree in any discipline. (e.g., Direction, Cinematography, Editing).
  • For PG Diploma Courses (TV Wing): Bachelor's Degree in any discipline. (e.g., Direction, Electronic Cinematography).
  • For Acting Course: 10+2 or equivalent. A Bachelor's degree is not mandatory.
  • Final year students are also eligible to apply.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no upper age limit** for any of the courses.

🎬Detailed Syllabus

The JET has a common Paper I and a course-specific Paper II.

  • Paper I (General Aptitude Test - MCQ): General Knowledge and questions related to Indian History, Culture, Art, Architecture, Music, Folk-art, Cinema, Television, and the Media industry.
  • Paper II (Course Specific): This is a descriptive paper to test the candidate's specific knowledge and aptitude for their chosen specialization. For example, it could involve writing a short story or scene (for Direction), visual composition tests (for Cinematography), or a performance test (for Acting).

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Joint Entrance Test (JET). All candidates appear for the written test (Paper I & Paper II).
  2. Step 2: Shortlisting. Candidates are shortlisted based on their JET scores.
  3. Step 3: Orientation & Interview / Audition. Shortlisted candidates are called to the FTII Pune campus for the final selection round, which includes interviews, creative workshops, and auditions (for acting).
  4. Step 4: Medical Test & Final Merit List. A final merit list is prepared based on the combined score of the written test and the interview/audition round.

💰Salary Potential (Highly Variable)

The film and media industry is not based on fixed salaries but on talent, networking, and project-based work.

  • Starting Potential: As an Assistant Director, Junior Editor, or Assistant Cinematographer on a film set, one might start with a stipend or salary of ₹30,000 to ₹70,000 per month, depending on the project's budget.
  • Experienced Professionals: There is no upper limit. Successful directors, cinematographers, and editors can earn several lakhs per project.

🎥Starting Post & Department

Graduates from FTII are highly sought-after. They typically start in assistant or trainee roles and quickly move up. Starting roles include:

  • Assistant Director (AD) in a film production.
  • Assistant Editor in a post-production house.
  • Cinematographer's Assistant or Focus Puller.
  • Professional Actor in films, television, and theatre.
  • Sound Recordist or Designer.

📋Job Profile

The job profile is creative, collaborative, and project-driven. It involves working in a team during pre-production (planning, scripting), production (shooting), and post-production (editing, sound mixing, VFX) to create a film, TV show, or web series.

🎁Facilities After Selection (At FTII)

  • Access to professional film studios, sound stages, and recording rooms.
  • Hands-on training with industry-standard cameras (like Arri, RED), lighting equipment, and sound gear.
  • Professional editing suites with software like Avid Media Composer and DaVinci Resolve.
  • A world-renowned faculty of experienced film professionals and scholars.
  • A rich cinematic culture with regular screenings and workshops by leading filmmakers.

🎯Scope

FTII is India's premier and most prestigious institute for film and television education. A diploma from FTII is a mark of excellence and provides unparalleled access to the Indian film industry. The institute's alumni include some of the country's most celebrated actors, directors, cinematographers, and editors, and the alumni network is extremely powerful.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The FTII-JET is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically in February or March.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official FTII-JET website. The process involves registration, filling the detailed application form for the chosen course, uploading documents, and paying the application fee.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Paper Type Max Marks
Paper I Objective (MCQ) - General Aptitude & GK 100
Paper II Descriptive / Course Specific 100

Scoring Pattern: Paper I has negative marking for incorrect answers. Paper II is evaluated subjectively by a panel of experts. The final selection gives weightage to both the written test and the interview/audition.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the written exam is 180 minutes (3 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results of the written test are typically declared within 2 to 3 months. The final merit list is released after the interview/audition process is complete.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹2,000 for one course for General category candidates, with an additional fee for applying to multiple courses. Fees are lower for reserved categories.

🌐Official Website

For all official notifications, prospectus, and application forms, always refer to the official FTII-JET website: applyadmission.net/jet... (The year in the URL changes).
