ℹ️Full Form

Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: A candidate who is currently studying in the 3rd or higher years of any undergraduate degree program OR has already completed any government-approved degree program in Engineering / Technology / Architecture / Science / Commerce / Arts is eligible.
  • No Marks Criteria: There is no minimum percentage requirement to appear for the GATE exam.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for candidates to appear for the GATE exam.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The exam consists of two main parts:

  • General Aptitude (GA): This section is common for all papers. It tests Verbal Ability (English grammar, vocabulary, reading comprehension) and Numerical Ability (quantitative aptitude, data interpretation, logical reasoning).
  • Core Subject Paper: This is the main part of the exam. The syllabus is specific to the engineering/science discipline chosen by the candidate (e.g., Computer Science, Mechanical, Civil, etc.) and is based on the standard undergraduate curriculum for that discipline.

⚙️Selection Process

GATE is a qualifying exam. The score is used for two primary purposes:

  1. Admission to Postgraduate Programs (M.E./M.Tech/Ph.D.):
    • Candidates use their GATE scorecard to apply to institutions like IITs, IISc Bangalore, NITs, etc.
    • Each institute then conducts its own admission process (like COAP or CCMT counselling) or interviews for final selection.
  2. Recruitment in Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs):
    • Many PSUs (like ONGC, IOCL, NTPC, BHEL) use GATE scores to shortlist candidates.
    • Shortlisted candidates are then called for Group Discussions (GD) and Personal Interviews (PI) for final selection.

💰Starting Salary / Stipend Potential

  • If joining a PSU: The starting salary is excellent. An Engineer/Officer Trainee in a Maharatna PSU can expect a CTC of approximately ₹15 Lakhs to ₹20 Lakhs per annum.
  • If pursuing M.Tech: Admitted students receive a monthly stipend from the Ministry of Education, which is currently ₹12,400 per month.

🚀Starting Post & Department

  • In PSUs: The starting designation is typically Executive Trainee or Engineer Trainee in the relevant technical department.
  • In Academia: The starting role is a Postgraduate Student (M.Tech Scholar or Ph.D. Fellow).

📋Job Profile (in a PSU)

The job profile of a PSU engineer is a blend of technical and managerial work. It involves project management, plant operations, maintenance, quality control, and working on large-scale national infrastructure projects. It is a stable and prestigious government-sector job.

🎁Facilities After Selection

  • In PSUs: Comprehensive benefits including housing/HRA, medical facilities for family, travel allowances, and performance-related pay.
  • In IITs/IISc: Access to world-class research labs, top academic faculty, extensive libraries, and a highly stimulating intellectual environment.

🎯Scope

GATE is the most important postgraduate entrance examination for engineers and science graduates in India. It has a powerful dual scope: it is the primary gateway for admission to prestigious Master's and Doctoral programs (M.Tech/Ph.D.) in institutions like IITs and IISc, and it is also the direct recruitment path for many coveted technical positions in India's top Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs).

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The GATE exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically held over multiple weekends in February.

📝How to Apply

The application process is conducted online through the official GATE Online Application Processing System (GOAPS). The exam is jointly organized by the seven IITs and IISc Bangalore on a rotational basis.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Section Distribution of Marks Total Marks
General Aptitude (GA) 10 Questions (5 x 1-mark + 5 x 2-marks) 15
Core Engineering Subject 55 Questions (25 x 1-mark + 30 x 2-marks) 85
Total 65 Questions 100

Question Types: The exam features Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ), Multiple Select Questions (MSQ), and Numerical Answer Type (NAT) questions.

Scoring Pattern: There is **negative marking** for incorrect answers in MCQs only (1/3rd for 1-mark questions, 2/3rd for 2-mark questions). There is no negative marking for MSQ and NAT questions.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 180 minutes (3 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared in the second or third week of March, approximately one month after the exam.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹1,800 for male candidates (General/OBC) and ₹900 for female candidates and reserved categories. The fee is higher for international candidates and during the extended registration period.

🌐Official Website

The official website changes each year depending on the organizing institute. For the current year, you can find it by searching "GATE [Year] Official Website" or starting at the main portal: gate.iitk.ac.in (link may change yearly).
