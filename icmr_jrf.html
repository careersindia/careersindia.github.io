DETAILS ABOUT ICMR-JRF EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Indian Council of Medical Research - Junior Research Fellowship (ICMR-JRF).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: M.Sc. / M.A. or equivalent degree in Life Sciences, Biomedical Sciences, Social Sciences, etc.
  • Marks: A minimum of 55% aggregate marks for General/EWS/OBC category and 50% for SC/ST/PwD candidates.
  • Final year students are also eligible to apply.

🎂Age Limit

The upper age limit is **28 years**. Age relaxation of up to 5 years is given for SC/ST/PwD/Female candidates and 3 years for OBC candidates.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The exam consists of two main parts:

  • Section A (Aptitude Section): Compulsory for all. Tests scientific phenomenon in everyday life, general knowledge in sciences, and common statistics.
  • Section B (Subject Specific Section): Candidates attempt questions from one of two streams:
    • Life Sciences: Microbiology, Physiology, Molecular Biology, Genetics, Human Nutrition, etc.
    • Social Sciences: Psychology, Sociology, Anthropology, Social Work, and Health Economics.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Appear for the ICMR-JRF Computer-Based Test.
  2. Step 2: Merit List Declaration. ICMR releases a merit list of qualified candidates based on the exam score.
  3. Step 3: Fellowship Activation. Qualified candidates can activate their JRF by enrolling for a Ph.D. program in any ICMR-affiliated or recognized medical research institute or university in India.

💰Stipend & Fellowship (During Ph.D.)

Qualified candidates receive a monthly stipend as per Government of India norms.

  • Junior Research Fellow (JRF): Approximately ₹37,000 per month for the first two years.
  • Senior Research Fellow (SRF): Approximately ₹42,000 per month for the remaining tenure, plus other allowances like HRA.

👨‍🔬Starting Post

This is an eligibility test for a fellowship. After qualifying, the starting designation is Junior Research Fellow (JRF) or Ph.D. Scholar.

📋Job Profile (NA)

NA (This is an eligibility test for a research fellowship. The profile is that of a Ph.D. researcher in biomedical or social sciences).

🎁Facilities After Selection (NA)

NA (Facilities depend on the institute where the candidate joins for their Ph.D.).

🎯Scope

ICMR-JRF is a prestigious national-level fellowship for students who wish to pursue a career in **biomedical and health research**. It is the primary pathway to a fully-funded Ph.D. at India's top medical colleges and ICMR's own research institutes. A Ph.D. backed by an ICMR fellowship is highly valued and leads to careers as a scientist, researcher, or professor in the medical field.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The ICMR-JRF exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically in June or July.

📝How to Apply

The application process is conducted online through the official website of the Postgraduate Institute of Medical Education & Research (PGIMER), Chandigarh, on behalf of ICMR.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Section Subject No. of Questions To be Attempted
Section A Aptitude (Compulsory) 50 50
Section B Life Sciences 100 75 (from either B or C)
Section C Social Sciences 100

Total Questions to Attempt: 125.    Total Marks: 125.

Scoring Pattern: +1 mark for every correct answer, -0.25 marks for every incorrect answer.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 120 minutes (2 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 1 to 2 months after the examination.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically ₹2,000 for General/EWS/OBC candidates and ₹1,500 for SC/ST/PwD candidates. This fee is subject to change.

🌐Official Website

For all official notifications and application forms, always refer to the official websites of ICMR and PGIMER Chandigarh: www.icmr.nic.in and pgimer.edu.in
