DETAILS ABOUT CA FOUNDATION

ℹ️Full Form

Chartered Accountant - Foundation Course. It is the entry-level test for the Chartered Accountancy course offered by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: A candidate can register for the CA Foundation course after passing the Class 10th examination.
  • Appearance Eligibility: A candidate can appear for the Foundation examination only after appearing in the Class 12th (or equivalent) examination.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for registering for or appearing in the CA Foundation exam.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The course is divided into four papers:

  • Paper 1: Accounting: Theoretical framework, accounting process, bank reconciliation, inventories, final accounts of sole proprietors, partnership accounts, and company accounts.
  • Paper 2: Business Laws: The Indian Contract Act, 1872; The Sale of Goods Act, 1930; The Indian Partnership Act, 1932; The Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008; and The Companies Act, 2013.
  • Paper 3: Quantitative Aptitude: Business Mathematics, Logical Reasoning, and Statistics.
  • Paper 4: Business Economics: Micro Economics, Macro Economics, and Indian Economic Development.

⚙️Path to Becoming a CA

Passing CA Foundation is the first step in a multi-stage professional journey:

  1. Pass CA Foundation.
  2. Register for and pass both groups of CA Intermediate.
  3. Complete a mandatory 3-year Articleship (Practical Training) under a practicing Chartered Accountant.
  4. Pass both groups of CA Final.
  5. Become a Member of ICAI and earn the title of 'Chartered Accountant'.

💰Starting Salary Potential (After Qualifying as a CA)

A newly qualified Chartered Accountant is highly sought after in the industry.

  • Average Campus Placement Salary: Approximately ₹9 Lakhs to ₹15 Lakhs per annum.
  • Highest Salary (Rank Holders/International): Can range from ₹30 Lakhs to ₹75 Lakhs per annum or more.

🏢Starting Post & Department (After Qualifying as a CA)

Qualified CAs begin their careers in various high-responsibility roles:

  • Audit & Assurance Associate in Big 4 firms (Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG).
  • Tax Consultant (Direct or Indirect Taxation).
  • Financial Analyst or Finance Manager in a company's finance department.
  • Credit Manager in banks.
  • They can also start their **own independent practice**.

🎁Benefits of Becoming a CA

  • One of the most prestigious and respected professional qualifications in commerce.

  • Global opportunities, as the qualification is recognized in many countries.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The CA Foundation exam is conducted **three times a year**.
  • Exam Months: Typically in June, September, and January.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must first register for the Foundation course with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) through their Self-Service Portal (SSP). After registration, they must fill a separate examination form for each attempt.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Paper Subject Type Max Marks
Paper 1 Accounting Subjective 100
Paper 2 Business Laws Subjective 100
Paper 3 Quantitative Aptitude Objective (MCQ) 100
Paper 4 Business Economics Objective (MCQ) 100

Scoring Pattern: To pass, a candidate must obtain a minimum of **40% marks in each paper** AND a minimum of **50% marks in the aggregate** of all four papers. There is **negative marking of 0.25 marks** for each wrong answer in the objective papers (Paper 3 & 4).

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The duration for each of the four papers is 3 hours (180 minutes).

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 1 to 2 months after the last examination date.

💸Fees

The fee structure involves a one-time course registration fee (around ₹9,000) and a separate examination application fee (around ₹1,500) for each attempt. These fees are subject to change by ICAI.
