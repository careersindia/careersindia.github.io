DETAILS ABOUT CTET EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET).

✅Eligibility

  • For Paper I (Classes I to V): Must have passed 10+2 with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed) OR 4-year Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed).
  • For Paper II (Classes VI to VIII): Must have a Graduation degree and passed or appearing in the final year of a 2-year D.El.Ed OR Graduation with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in a 1-year Bachelor in Education (B.Ed).
  • (Note: Several other combinations exist. Candidates must check the official notification for detailed eligibility criteria.)

🎂Age Limit

There is **no upper age limit** to apply for the CTET exam.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • Child Development and Pedagogy: Focuses on educational psychology of teaching and learning for the relevant age group (6-11 years for Paper I, 11-14 years for Paper II).
  • Language I & Language II: Two different languages must be chosen. The test assesses comprehension, pedagogy of language development.
  • Mathematics (Paper I): Foundational concepts of geometry, shapes, numbers, problem-solving, and pedagogical issues.
  • Environmental Studies (EVS - Paper I): Concepts and pedagogical issues related to family, food, shelter, water, travel, etc.
  • Mathematics & Science OR Social Studies (Paper II): Candidates choose one. The syllabus is based on topics from the Class VI-VIII NCERT curriculum.

⚙️Selection Process

CTET is an **eligibility test**, not a recruitment exam. The process is simple:

  1. Step 1: Appear for the CTET Written Exam. A candidate can appear for Paper I, Paper II, or both.
  2. Step 2: Qualify the Exam. Candidates need to score 60% (90 out of 150 marks) or more to be declared qualified. Relaxations apply for reserved categories.
  3. Step 3: Receive Eligibility Certificate. Qualified candidates receive a CTET Eligibility Certificate from CBSE, which is now valid for a lifetime.
  4. Step 4: Apply for Teaching Jobs. The candidate can use this certificate to apply for teaching vacancies in Central Government schools (KVS, NVS, etc.) and other schools that accept CTET scores.

💰Starting Salary Potential (After Securing a Job)

The salary depends on the government or private school one joins. For Central Government schools like KVS/NVS:

  • Primary Teacher (PRT): Approximately ₹55,000 - ₹60,000 per month (in a metro city).
  • Trained Graduate Teacher (TGT): Approximately ₹65,000 - ₹75,000 per month (in a metro city).

🧑‍🏫Starting Post & Department (After Recruitment)

Based on the CTET paper qualified, a candidate can apply for:

  • Primary Teacher (PRT): For teaching Classes I to V.
  • Trained Graduate Teacher (TGT): For teaching Classes VI to VIII (or up to X in some schools).

These posts are in educational institutions like Kendriya Vidyalayas (KVS), Navodaya Vidyalayas (NVS), Army Public Schools (APS), and private schools following the CBSE board.

📋Job Profile

The job profile of a teacher involves lesson planning, classroom instruction, student assessment, creating a positive learning environment, engaging in parent-teacher meetings, and contributing to the overall development of students.

🎁Facilities After Selection (As a Govt. Teacher)

  • Job security and a stable career.
  • A respected position in society.
  • Defined pay scales with regular increments (as per Pay Commission).
  • Medical benefits (e.g., CGHS) and leave entitlements (maternity, child care leave, etc.).
  • Opportunities for professional development and training.

🎯Scope

A CTET certificate is the mandatory eligibility criterion for applying to teaching positions in all Central Government schools. It is also a benchmark for quality for many private schools, which often give preference to CTET-qualified candidates. It is the essential first step for anyone aspiring to a career as a teacher in the K-12 segment in India.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The CTET exam is conducted twice a year.
  • Exam Months: Typically in July and December.

📝How to Apply

The application process is conducted online through the official CTET website, managed by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE). Candidates need to register, fill the form, upload documents, and pay the fee.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Both Paper I and Paper II are offline, pen-and-paper (OMR-based) tests.

Subject No. of Questions Maximum Marks
Child Development & Pedagogy 30 30
Language I (compulsory) 30 30
Language II (compulsory) 30 30
Mathematics (for Paper I) / Maths & Science OR Social Studies (for Paper II) 30 (Paper I) / 60 (Paper II) 30 (Paper I) / 60 (Paper II)
Environmental Studies (for Paper I only) 30 30
Total 150 150

Scoring Pattern: +1 mark for every correct answer. There is **no negative marking** for incorrect answers.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The duration for each paper (Paper I and Paper II) is 150 minutes (2.5 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 3 to 4 weeks after the examination date.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically ₹1,000 for one paper or ₹1,200 for both papers for General/OBC candidates. The fee is halved for SC/ST/PwD candidates.

🌐Official Website

For all official notifications, application forms, and results, always refer to the official CTET website: ctet.nic.in
