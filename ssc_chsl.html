DETAILS ABOUT SSC-CHSL EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Staff Selection Commission - Combined Higher Secondary Level (10+2) Examination.

✅Eligibility

  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India (or other eligible categories as per Govt. norms).
  • Academic Qualification: Must have passed 12th Standard or equivalent examination from a recognized Board or University.

🎂Age Limit

The age limit is **18 to 27 years**. Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/PwD/Ex-Servicemen) as per government rules.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • General Intelligence: Verbal and non-verbal reasoning, including analogy, classification, series, coding-decoding, and spatial visualization.
  • General Awareness: Static GK (History, Geography, Polity, Economics, Science) and Current Affairs. Emphasis is on general awareness from a broad perspective.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Basic arithmetic skills including Number Systems, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry, and Statistical Charts. (Class 10th level).
  • English Language: Spot the Error, Fill in the Blanks, Synonyms/Antonyms, Spelling/Detecting mis-spelt words, Idioms & Phrases, Comprehension Passage.

⚙️Selection Process

The selection process is conducted in two tiers:

  1. Tier-I: Computer Based Examination (CBE). An objective multiple-choice exam that is qualifying in nature.
  2. Tier-II: Computer Based Examination (CBE). This stage is for merit calculation. It consists of objective multiple-choice questions and a Skill Test/Typing Test module which is qualifying in nature.
  3. Step 3: Document Verification. Candidates are called for document verification based on their performance in both tiers.

💰Starting Salary Potential

The salary varies based on the post. For a post in a metropolitan city, the in-hand starting salary is approximately:

  • Lower Division Clerk (LDC)/JSA: ₹30,000 - ₹35,000 per month.
  • Data Entry Operator (DEO): ₹35,000 - ₹40,000 per month.

🏢Starting Post & Department

SSC CHSL recruits for Group 'C' posts in various Ministries and Departments of the Government of India. The main posts are:

  • Lower Division Clerk (LDC) / Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA)
  • Data Entry Operator (DEO)
  • Data Entry Operator (Grade A)

📋Job Profile

The job profile is primarily clerical and administrative in nature.

  • LDC/JSA: Maintaining files, drafting letters, data entry, and assisting senior officials in day-to-day office work.
  • DEO: Primarily responsible for typing and data entry on computers, preparing reports, and maintaining official records.

🎁Facilities After Selection

  • A secure central government job with a stable career path.
  • Pension benefits under the National Pension System (NPS).
  • Medical facilities via the Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS).
  • Paid leaves, Leave Travel Concession (LTC), and other allowances.

🎯Scope

SSC CHSL is one of the most popular government job exams in India after the 12th standard. It offers an excellent opportunity to enter the government sector at a young age. There is a well-defined promotional hierarchy, and through departmental exams, an LDC can be promoted to UDC, Assistant Section Officer, and higher posts over time.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The SSC-CHSL exam is generally conducted once a year.
  • Exam Months: The exam schedule varies, but the Tier-I exam is typically held mid-year.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the new official website of the Staff Selection Commission, using their One-Time Registration (OTR) profile.

💸Fees

The application fee is ₹100 for General/OBC male candidates. **Women candidates and candidates belonging to SC, ST, PwD, and Ex-servicemen categories are exempted from fee payment.**
