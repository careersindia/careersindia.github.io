DETAILS ABOUT CMA INTERMEDIATE

ℹ️Full Form

Cost and Management Accountant - Intermediate Course. This is the second level of the CMA course offered by ICMAI.

✅Eligibility

There are two routes to enter the CMA Intermediate course:

  • Foundation Route: Must have passed the CMA Foundation Examination.
  • Direct Entry Route:
    • Must have passed a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline (except Fine Arts).
    • Candidates who have cleared the Intermediate level of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) or the Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI).

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for registering for or appearing in the CMA Intermediate exam.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The course is divided into two groups with four papers each (total of eight papers):

  • Group I: Financial Accounting, Laws and Ethics, Direct Taxation, and Cost Accounting.
  • Group II: Operations Management & Strategic Management, Cost & Management Accounting and Financial Management, Indirect Taxation, and Company Accounts & Audit.

⚙️Path to Becoming a CMA

Passing CMA Intermediate is the crucial second step in the CMA journey:

  1. Pass CMA Foundation (or be eligible for Direct Entry).
  2. Clear both groups of CMA Intermediate.
  3. Complete Practical Training (15 months).
  4. Clear both groups of CMA Final.
  5. Become a Member of ICMAI.

💰Salary Potential (NA)

NA. This is a student-level exam. However, after clearing the Intermediate level, candidates begin their practical training, during which they receive a **monthly stipend**. The stipend amount varies by company but provides valuable work experience.

🏢Starting Post & Department (NA)

After passing CMA Intermediate, the candidate's designation during training is **"Cost Trainee"** or **"Management Trainee"** in the finance, accounts, or costing department of a company.

📋Job Profile of a Cost Trainee

The practical training is hands-on. The profile involves:

  • Working in cost accounting, budgeting, and financial planning.
  • Assisting in the preparation of cost sheets and variance analysis.
  • Learning about direct and indirect taxation compliance.
  • Participating in internal audits and financial reporting.

🎁Facilities (NA)

NA (This is a professional exam, not a job).

🎯Scope

Clearing the CMA Intermediate exam is a significant achievement that signifies a strong, working knowledge of cost and management accounting. It is the mandatory bridge to the CMA Final level and the gateway to the crucial 15-month practical training, where theoretical knowledge is applied in a real-world corporate environment.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The CMA Intermediate exam is conducted **twice a year**.
  • Exam Months: In June and December.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must first register for the Intermediate course with ICMAI through their official website. After the required study period, they must fill a separate examination form online for each attempt.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Paper Type Max Marks
All 8 Papers Objective (MCQ) & Subjective 100 per paper

Scoring Pattern: To pass a group, a candidate must obtain a minimum of **40% marks in each paper** AND a minimum of **50% marks in the aggregate** of that group. There is **no negative marking** in the objective (MCQ) part.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The duration for each of the eight papers is 3 hours (180 minutes).

⏳Result Time

The results for the June and December exams are typically declared by the last week of **August** and the last week of **February**, respectively (within 2 months).

💸Fees

The fee structure involves a one-time course registration fee (around ₹23,100) and a separate examination fee for each attempt (around ₹1,200 for one group or ₹2,400 for both). These fees are subject to change by ICMAI.
