DETAILS ABOUT ICAR AIEEA-UG

ℹ️Full Form

Indian Council of Agricultural Research - All India Entrance Examination for Admission (Undergraduate).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: Must have passed the 10+2 or equivalent examination from a recognized board.
  • Subjects in 10+2: Must have studied and passed in at least three subjects from the following combinations: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics (PCM), Physics, Chemistry, Biology (PCB), Physics, Chemistry, Agriculture (PCA), or Agriculture, Biology, Chemistry (ABC).

🎂Age Limit

The candidate must have completed a minimum of **16 years** of age as on 31st August of the admission year. There is no upper age limit.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The syllabus for each subject (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Agriculture) is based on the Class XI and XII curriculum, similar to NEET and JEE Main.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Appear for ICAR AIEEA-UG Exam. This is a Computer-Based Test (CBT).
  2. Step 2: Rank Card Release. The NTA releases a rank card based on the percentile score of the candidate.
  3. Step 3: Centralized Online Counselling. Candidates with a valid rank participate in online counselling for seat allotment to various Agricultural Universities based on their rank and choices.

💰Scholarship & Salary Potential

During B.Sc. program: Meritorious students admitted through ICAR are awarded the National Talent Scholarship (NTS) of approximately ₹3,000 per month.

After Graduation:

  • Government Sector (e.g., Agricultural Officer): Starting salary of ₹50,000 - ₹70,000 per month.
  • Private Sector (e.g., in seed/fertilizer companies): Starting salary of ₹4 Lakhs to ₹8 Lakhs per annum.
  • Bank AFO (Specialist Officer): Starting salary of ₹65,000 - ₹75,000 per month.

🌱Starting Post & Career Paths

After a B.Sc. in Agriculture or allied sciences, graduates can pursue various careers:

  • Agricultural Officer (AO) in State governments.
  • Agricultural Field Officer (AFO) in Public Sector Banks.
  • Scientist in ICAR institutions (after M.Sc. and NET/ARS).
  • Field Officer/Sales Officer in private seed, fertilizer, and pesticide companies.
  • Pursuing higher studies (M.Sc. Agriculture).

📋Job Profile (NA)

NA (This is an entrance exam for an undergraduate course).

🎁Facilities After Selection (in Agricultural Universities)

  • A curriculum with a strong focus on practical, field-based learning.
  • Access to large university farms, research stations, and laboratories.
  • Hands-on training through the RAWE (Rural Agricultural Work Experience) program.
  • Experienced faculty and scientists.
  • Hostel facilities on campus.

🎯Scope

ICAR-AIEEA-UG is the most prestigious national-level entrance exam for admission to Bachelor's degree programs in agriculture and allied sciences (like Horticulture, Forestry, Fisheries). It is the gateway to the 15% All India Quota seats in top State Agricultural Universities (SAUs) and 100% seats in National Dairy Research Institute (NDRI) Karnal, RLB Central Agricultural University Jhansi, etc. A degree in agriculture offers diverse and stable career opportunities in the evergreen agricultural sector.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically in June or July.

📝How to Apply

The application process is managed by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Candidates must apply online through the official NTA ICAR website.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Subject Number of Questions Maximum Marks
Subject 1 (e.g., Physics) 50 200
Subject 2 (e.g., Chemistry) 50 200
Subject 3 (e.g., Maths/Biology) 50 200
Total 150 600

Scoring Pattern: +4 marks for every correct answer, -1 mark for every incorrect answer.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 150 minutes (2 hours and 30 minutes).

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 3 to 4 weeks after the examination date.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹800 for General category candidates, with lower fees for reserved categories. This fee is subject to change.
