DETAILS ABOUT SRFTI-JET EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Satyajit Ray Film and Television Institute (SRFTI) - Joint Entrance Test (JET). This is a common entrance exam for admission to both SRFTI, Kolkata and FTII, Pune.

✅Eligibility

  • For PG Diploma Courses (Film & TV): Must hold a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized university.
  • For Animation Cinema Course: A Bachelor's Degree is required.
  • Final year students are also eligible to apply for all courses.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no upper age limit** for applying to any of the courses.

🎬Detailed Syllabus

The JET exam consists of two papers to test general and specific aptitudes.

  • Paper I (General Aptitude Test): Objective and Multiple Select Questions (MSQ) on General Knowledge, Indian History, Art, Culture, Cinema, Music, and Media Awareness.
  • Paper II (Course Specific): A subjective/descriptive paper designed to test the candidate's aptitude for their chosen specialization. This can involve creative writing, drawing, story visualization, or critical analysis.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Joint Entrance Test (JET). All candidates appear for the written test.
  2. Step 2: Shortlisting. Candidates are shortlisted based on their JET scores for the next round.
  3. Step 3: Orientation & Interview. Shortlisted candidates are called to the SRFTI Kolkata campus for a rigorous final selection round that includes an orientation, interactive sessions, and a personal interview.
  4. Step 4: Medical Test & Final Merit List. A final merit list is prepared based on the combined weightage of the written test and the interview round.

💰Salary Potential (Highly Variable)

The film industry is project-based. Graduates from SRFTI are highly respected and command good remuneration based on their skills and the project budget.

  • Starting Potential: As an assistant in various departments (Direction, Cinematography, Editing), one can expect a monthly income of ₹35,000 to ₹80,000.
  • Experienced Professionals: There is no ceiling. Successful directors, cinematographers, and editors earn lakhs per project.

🎥Starting Post & Department

Graduates begin their careers in key creative and technical roles, usually as assistants, such as:

  • Assistant Director
  • Assistant Cinematographer / Camera Assistant
  • Assistant Editor / Edit Trainee
  • Sound Recordist / Assistant Sound Designer
  • Animator / Storyboard Artist

🎁Facilities After Selection (At SRFTI)

  • A sprawling, green campus with a rich cinematic atmosphere.
  • Professional-grade film studios, sound recording stages, and post-production facilities.
  • Hands-on training with industry-standard film cameras, sound equipment, and editing software.
  • A faculty of experienced film professionals and an excellent library with a vast collection of classic and contemporary films.
  • Fully-funded student diploma film projects.

🎯Scope

SRFTI, along with FTII, is one of the two premier, autonomous film schools funded by the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting, Govt. of India. A diploma from SRFTI is a mark of creative and technical excellence, providing an unparalleled launchpad into the world of Indian and international cinema. The institute's alumni network is extremely influential in the film and television industry.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The SRFTI-JET is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: The written test is typically held in February or March.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official JET website. The application process is centralized for both FTII and SRFTI, where candidates can choose their preferred institute and course.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Paper Type Maximum Marks
Paper I Objective (MCQ/MSQ) 100
Paper II Descriptive / Course Specific 100

Scoring Pattern: Paper I has negative marking for incorrect answers. Paper II is evaluated subjectively. The final selection gives weightage to both the written test and the interview round.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the written exam is 180 minutes (3 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results of the written test are typically declared within 2 to 3 months. The final merit list is released after the interview/orientation process is complete.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹2,000 for one course for General category candidates, with an additional fee for applying to multiple courses. Fees are lower for reserved categories.
