DETAILS ABOUT NCHMCT-JEE EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

National Council for Hotel Management and Catering Technology Joint Entrance Examination (NCHMCT-JEE).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: Must have passed or be appearing in the 10+2 or its equivalent examination.
  • Compulsory Subject: English must be one of the compulsory subjects in the 10+2 curriculum.

🎂Age Limit

Yes, there is an upper age limit. It is **25 years** for General/OBC/EWS candidates and **28 years** for SC/ST candidates as on July 1st of the admission year.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • Numerical Ability and Analytical Aptitude: Basic mathematics of Class 10th level, including Percentage, Profit & Loss, Time & Work, and simple data interpretation.
  • Reasoning and Logical Deduction: Verbal and non-verbal reasoning, series, analogies, syllogisms, and puzzles.
  • General Knowledge & Current Affairs: Static GK and awareness of national and international current events.
  • English Language: Vocabulary, Grammar, Reading Comprehension, Error Spotting, and Para Jumbles.
  • Aptitude for Service Sector: This unique section tests interpersonal skills, situational judgment, and a service-oriented mindset.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Appear for NCHMCT-JEE. This is a single, computer-based test (CBT).
  2. Step 2: Rank List Declaration. The NTA releases an All India Rank (AIR) list based on the candidate's score.
  3. Step 3: Centralized Online Counselling. Candidates with a valid rank participate in a centralized online counselling process for choice filling and seat allotment to the various Institutes of Hotel Management (IHMs) across the country.

💰Starting Salary Potential (After B.Sc. HHA)

Graduates from top IHMs are recruited by leading hotel chains and hospitality companies.

  • Average Starting Salary: Approximately ₹4 Lakhs to ₹7 Lakhs per annum as a Management Trainee.
  • After a few years: Can grow rapidly to ₹10 Lakhs to ₹15 Lakhs per annum in managerial roles. General Managers of 5-star hotels can earn significantly more.

🏨Starting Post & Department

Graduates typically start their career as a Management Trainee or Operational Trainee in one of the core hotel departments:

  • Food & Beverage (F&B) Service
  • Front Office
  • Housekeeping
  • Food Production (Kitchen)

📋Job Profile

The job of a hotel management professional is dynamic and guest-focused. It involves managing day-to-day operations, overseeing staff, ensuring excellent customer service, handling guest relations, managing budgets, and upholding the brand standards of the hotel.

🎁Facilities After Selection (in an IHM)

  • A curriculum with a strong emphasis on practical, hands-on training.
  • State-of-the-art infrastructure, including training kitchens, bakeries, model hotel rooms, and front office labs.
  • Mandatory industrial training in top hotels as part of the course.
  • Experienced faculty from the hospitality industry.
  • Strong placement cells with ties to all major national and international hotel chains.

🎯Scope

NCHMCT-JEE is the premier entrance exam for admission to the B.Sc. in Hospitality & Hotel Administration program offered by the government-run Institutes of Hotel Management (IHMs). An IHM degree is the gold standard for hospitality education in India, opening up vast career opportunities in luxury hotels, airlines, cruise lines, event management, and the retail sector, both in India and abroad.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The NCHMCT-JEE is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically in April or May.

📝How to Apply

The application process is managed by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Candidates must apply online through the official NTA NCHMCT website. The process involves registration, form filling, document upload, and fee payment.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Section Number of Questions Maximum Marks
Numerical Ability & Analytical Aptitude 30 120
Reasoning & Logical Deduction 30 120
General Knowledge & Current Affairs 30 120
English Language 60 240
Aptitude for Service Sector 50 200
Total 200 800

Scoring Pattern: +4 marks for every correct answer, -1 mark for every incorrect answer.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 180 minutes (3 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 4 to 5 weeks after the examination.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹1,000 for General/OBC candidates and ₹450-700 for reserved categories. This fee is subject to change.
