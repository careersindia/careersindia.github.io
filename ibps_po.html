DETAILS ABOUT IBPS PO EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Institute of Banking Personnel Selection - Probationary Officer (IBPS PO).

✅Eligibility

  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India (or other eligible categories as per Govt. norms).
  • Academic Qualification: A Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a University recognised by the Govt. of India.
  • Computer Literacy: Operating and working knowledge in computer systems is mandatory.

🎂Age Limit

The candidate must be between **20 and 30 years** of age. Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/PwD) as per government rules.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • Reasoning & Computer Aptitude: Puzzles, Seating Arrangements, Syllogism, Blood Relations, Coding-Decoding, and basic computer knowledge.
  • Quantitative Aptitude / Data Analysis & Interpretation: Data Interpretation (DI), Number Series, Simplification, and Arithmetic problems.
  • English Language: Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Error Spotting, Para Jumbles.
  • General/Economy/Banking Awareness: Current Affairs (especially last 6 months), Banking & Financial Awareness, Static GK.
  • Descriptive Paper: Letter Writing and Essay.

⚙️Selection Process

The selection is a three-phase process:

  1. Phase-I: Preliminary Examination. An online objective test that is qualifying in nature.
  2. Phase-II: Main Examination. Consists of an online objective test and a descriptive test. Marks from this phase are considered for shortlisting for the interview.
  3. Phase-III: Interview. Candidates shortlisted from the Main Examination are called for a personal interview conducted by the participating banks.

The final merit list is prepared based on the combined score of the Main Examination and the Interview.

💰Starting Salary Potential

An IBPS PO is a Scale-I Officer. The starting in-hand salary, including all allowances like HRA, DA, etc., in a metropolitan city is approximately ₹60,000 - ₹70,000 per month.

🏦Starting Post & Department

The starting post is **Probationary Officer (PO)**, which is an Assistant Manager (Scale-I) trainee post in one of the participating Public Sector Banks (PSBs).

📋Job Profile

A Probationary Officer undergoes a 1-2 year training period. The role is a generalist officer profile involving:

  • Supervising clerical work.
  • Customer service and handling grievances.
  • Processing loans and opening new accounts.
  • Increasing bank business through marketing of products.
  • General banking and administrative tasks.

🎁Facilities After Selection

  • A prestigious and secure officer-level job in the public sector.
  • Pension benefits under the National Pension System (NPS).
  • Medical benefits, Leave Travel Concession (LTC).
  • Leased accommodation or House Rent Allowance (HRA).
  • Access to concessional rates on loans (housing, vehicle, etc.).

🎯Scope

IBPS PO is the most sought-after entry route to a managerial career in Public Sector Banks. It offers one of the fastest career growth paths in the government sector. A PO can be promoted to Manager (Scale-II) in just 3-5 years and can rise to the level of General Manager or even Executive Director of a bank.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The IBPS PO exam is conducted annually.
  • Exam Months: The Preliminary exam is typically held in October, and the Main exam is held in November.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official IBPS website. The process involves registration, filling the application form, uploading documents, and paying the application fee online.
