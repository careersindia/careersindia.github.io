NEET-PG – National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (Postgraduate)

Eligibility
Candidates must possess an MBBS degree or Provisional MBBS Pass Certificate and a permanent or provisional registration certificate of MBBS qualification issued by the MCI/SMC. Completion of a one-year internship is mandatory.
Scope
Admission to MD/MS and PG Diploma courses in medical colleges across India, except for institutions like AIIMS, PGIMER, JIPMER, NIMHANS, and Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute.
How to Apply
Online application via the official website of the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS).
Exam Structure
Computer-Based Test (CBT) with 200 multiple-choice questions from the MBBS curriculum. Duration: 3 hours and 30 minutes.
Score Pattern
+4 marks for each correct answer; -1 mark for each incorrect answer (25% negative marking).
Fees
Approximately ₹4,250 for General/OBC category; ₹3,250 for SC/ST/PwD.
Exam Timing
Conducted once a year, with the date scheduled by NBEMS, typically in the first half of the year.
Results
Announced on the official NBEMS website, usually within a few weeks of the exam date.
