ℹ️Full Form

Employees' Provident Fund Organisation - Social Security Assistant (SSA).

✅Eligibility

  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India.
  • Academic Qualification: Must hold a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university.
  • Skill Requirement: Must possess a typing speed of 35 words per minute in English or 30 words per minute in Hindi on a computer.

🎂Age Limit

The candidate must be between **18 and 27 years** of age. Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/Ex-Servicemen) as per government rules.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • General Aptitude (Reasoning): Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Syllogism, Blood Relations, Coding-Decoding.
  • General Knowledge/General Awareness: Current Affairs, Indian History, Geography, Economy, Polity, and basic Computer knowledge.
  • Quantitative Ability: Data Interpretation, Simplification, Number Series, and Arithmetic problems.
  • General English: Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Error Spotting, and Vocabulary.
  • Computer Literacy: Basics of computer, hardware, software, networking, and MS Office.

⚙️Selection Process

The selection is a two-phase process:

  1. Phase-I: Computer Based Examination. A single online objective test. Marks from this phase are used for the final merit list.
  2. Phase-II: Computer Skill Test (Typing Test). This test is qualifying in nature. Candidates need to meet the minimum speed and accuracy criteria.

💰Starting Salary Potential

SSC SSA is a Group 'C' post in Pay Level-5. The in-hand starting salary, including allowances in a metropolitan city, is approximately ₹45,000 - ₹55,000 per month.

🏢Starting Post & Department

The starting post is **Social Security Assistant (SSA)** in the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), under the Ministry of Labour and Employment, Govt. of India.

📋Job Profile

The job of an SSA is a desk-based, clerical role focused on data management. Responsibilities include:

  • Data entry and maintaining records of PF account holders.
  • Processing and settling claims (withdrawal, transfer, etc.).
  • Updating passbooks and addressing queries of members.
  • Supporting senior officers in administrative tasks.

🎁Facilities After Selection

  • A secure job in a central government-backed organization.
  • Pension benefits under the National Pension System (NPS).
  • Medical benefits and other standard government allowances like HRA, DA, etc.
  • Good work-life balance with a five-day work week.

🎯Scope

A career as an EPFO SSA offers a stable career with a clear promotional path. An SSA can be promoted to **Senior Social Security Assistant (Sr. SSA)** and then to **Section Supervisor**. Furthermore, there are opportunities to appear for departmental exams to get promoted to the officer cadre post of **Enforcement Officer (EO) / Accounts Officer (AO)**.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: This exam is **not conducted annually**. It is held based on the vacancies reported by EPFO, often with a gap of a few years.
  • Exam Months: The exam schedule varies greatly depending on the recruitment cycle.

📝How to Apply

The application process is managed by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Candidates must apply online through the official NTA or EPFO recruitment website when the notification is released.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Subject No. of Questions Maximum Marks
General Aptitude 30 120
General Knowledge/Awareness 30 120
Quantitative Ability 30 120
General English with Comprehension 50 200
Computer Literacy 10 40
Total 150 600

Scoring Pattern: +4 marks for every correct answer, -1 mark for every incorrect answer.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the computer-based exam is 150 minutes (2 hours and 30 minutes).

⏳Result Time

The results for the written test are typically declared within 1 to 2 months after the examination.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹700 for General/OBC candidates. **Female candidates and candidates belonging to SC, ST, PwD, and Ex-servicemen categories are exempted from fee payment.**

🌐Official Website

For all official notifications and application forms, always refer to the official EPFO or NTA recruitment websites, such as: recruitment.nta.nic.in
