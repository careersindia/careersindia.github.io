ℹ️Full Form

Life Insurance Corporation of India - Assistant Administrative Officer (LIC AAO).

✅Eligibility

  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India.
  • Educational Qualification: A Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized Indian University/Institution. (Specific qualifications required for specialist posts like CA, Actuarial, Rajbhasha, etc.).

🎂Age Limit

The candidate must be between **21 and 30 years** of age. Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories as per government norms.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The syllabus is similar to other major banking and insurance exams.

  • Reasoning Ability: Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Syllogism, Inequality, Blood Relation, Coding-Decoding, Direction Sense.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Data Interpretation (DI), Number Series, Simplification/Approximation, Quadratic Equations, and Arithmetic problems.
  • English Language: Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Error Spotting, Sentence Improvement, Para Jumbles.
  • General Awareness: Current Affairs (last 6 months), Banking & Insurance Awareness, Static GK, Financial Awareness.
  • Insurance and Financial Market Awareness: (For Mains) History of Insurance, IRDAI, Insurance terms, Financial Market concepts.

⚙️Selection Process

The selection process is conducted in three phases:

  1. Phase I: Preliminary Examination. An online objective test. This is a qualifying stage.
  2. Phase II: Main Examination. An online examination consisting of both objective and descriptive tests. Marks from this phase are considered for the final merit list.
  3. Phase III: Interview. Candidates shortlisted from the Main Examination are called for a personal interview.
  4. Final Step: Medical Examination. A pre-recruitment medical examination is conducted before final appointment.

💰Starting Salary Potential

The LIC AAO is a well-paying job. The starting basic pay is ₹53,600 per month in the scale. The total starting emoluments, including allowances and benefits, in a metropolitan area are approximately ₹92,870 per month.

🏢Starting Post & Department

The starting post is Assistant Administrative Officer (AAO). AAOs are recruited for various departments within LIC, including:

  • Generalist
  • IT (Information Technology)
  • Chartered Accountant (CA)
  • Actuarial
  • Rajbhasha (Official Language)

📋Job Profile

An AAO's role is primarily administrative. Key responsibilities include managing and overseeing policies, processing claims, interacting with clients, inspecting documents, and assisting other officials in day-to-day operations. It is a desk-based officer-level job.

🎁Facilities After Selection

As an employee of a prestigious Public Sector Undertaking, an AAO is entitled to many benefits:

  • Defined Contributory Pension Scheme.
  • Gratuity, LTC (Leave Travel Concession).
  • Medical Benefits and Group Insurance.
  • Housing Loan at concessional rates.
  • Vehicle Loan and other advances.
  • 5-day work week (Saturday and Sunday off).

🎯Scope

LIC AAO is a stable and secure career with excellent growth prospects. There is a well-defined promotion channel, and an AAO can rise through the ranks to become an Administrative Officer (AO), Assistant Divisional Manager (ADM), Divisional Manager (DM), and even higher positions within the corporation's hierarchy.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The LIC AAO exam is **not conducted annually**. It is held based on the vacancies and requirements of the corporation, often with a gap of 1-3 years between notifications.
  • Exam Months: The notification and exam dates vary. Candidates must check the official website for updates.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the "Careers" section of the official LIC website. The process involves online registration, filling the application form, uploading a photograph/signature, and paying the application fee.
