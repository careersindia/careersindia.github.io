ℹ️Full Form

NMAT by GMAC™ (Graduate Management Admission Council).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: Must hold a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized university.
  • Marks: A minimum of 50% aggregate marks in the undergraduate degree.
  • Final Year Students: Candidates appearing for the final year of their Bachelor’s degree are also eligible to apply.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for candidates to appear for the NMAT exam.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • Language Skills: Reading Comprehension, Para Jumbles, Error Identification, Analogies, Prepositions, and Fill in the Blanks.
  • Logical Reasoning: Critical Reasoning (Assumptions, Conclusions, Arguments), Analytical Puzzles, Input-Output, Blood Relations, and other standard reasoning topics.
  • Quantitative Skills: Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, and Data Interpretation (Tables, Graphs, Charts).

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: NMAT by GMAC™ Exam. Candidates appear for the computer-adaptive test within a long testing window.
  2. Step 2: Scorecard Release. Candidates can view their scores within 48 hours of the exam. They can choose to send their scores to up to 5 B-schools for free.
  3. Step 3: Apply to B-Schools. Candidates must apply individually to their target business schools that accept NMAT scores.
  4. Step 4: CD/PI Round. Each participating institute shortlists candidates based on their NMAT scores and conducts its own selection process, which typically includes a Case Discussion (CD) and a Personal Interview (PI).

💰Salary Potential (After MBA)

Salaries depend heavily on the college. NMIMS Mumbai, the flagship institute accepting NMAT scores, has placements comparable to top IIMs.

  • Average Salary Package (NMIMS Mumbai): Approximately ₹25 Lakhs to ₹30 Lakhs per annum.
  • Average Salary Package (Other top colleges like XIMB, K J Somaiya): Approximately ₹15 Lakhs to ₹20 Lakhs per annum.

🎯Scope

NMAT is a major national-level MBA entrance exam, primarily known as the mandatory gateway to all campuses of NMIMS (Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies). Its score is also accepted by a growing list of other top-tier business schools in India (like XIMB, K J Somaiya, VIT Vellore) and abroad. The exam's student-friendly features like multiple attempts and no negative marking make it a popular choice among MBA aspirants.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The NMAT exam is held over a long **testing window** of about 70-75 days.
  • Multiple Attempts: A candidate can attempt the NMAT a maximum of **three times** during this window (one main attempt and two retakes).
  • Exam Window: Typically from **October to December** each year.

📝How to Apply

The application process is managed by GMAC. Candidates must register online through the official NMAT by GMAC™ website. They can schedule their test date and location at their convenience within the testing window.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Section Number of Questions Sectional Time Limit Score Range
Language Skills 36 28 Minutes 12 - 120
Logical Reasoning 36 40 Minutes 12 - 120
Quantitative Skills 36 40 Minutes 12 - 120
Total 108 108 Minutes 36 - 360

Scoring Pattern: NMAT is a computer-adaptive test. The difficulty of the next question is based on your answer to the previous one. There is **no negative marking** for incorrect answers.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 108 minutes (1 hour and 48 minutes), with strict sectional time limits.

⏳Result Time

This is a key feature of NMAT. The unofficial scorecard is available on-screen immediately after finishing the exam, and the official scorecard is released within **48 hours** in the candidate's login portal.

💸Fees

The application fee is relatively high. It is typically around ₹2,800 for the main attempt, with an additional fee for each retake and for sending scores to additional B-schools. This fee is subject to change.
