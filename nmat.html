NMAT – NMIMS Management Aptitude Test

Eligibility
Candidates must have a bachelor's degree (10+2+3 or 4 years) in any discipline from a recognized university with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks.
Scope
Admission to MBA and other postgraduate management programs at NMIMS University and other leading business schools in India and abroad that accept NMAT scores.
How to Apply
Online registration through the official NMAT by GMAC website.
Exam Structure
Computer-adaptive test with three sections: Language Skills, Quantitative Skills, and Logical Reasoning. Total of 108 questions to be answered in 120 minutes, with individual time limits for each section.
Score Pattern
No negative marking for incorrect answers. Scores are scaled for each section and totaled.
Fees
Approximately ₹2,800 + taxes. Additional fees for retakes and applying to schools.
Exam Timing
Conducted over a 70-75 day testing window, typically from October to December. Candidates can schedule their own test date and attempt the exam up to three times.
Results
Score reports are typically available within 48 hours of the exam attempt.
