DETAILS ABOUT JEST EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Joint Entrance Screening Test (JEST).

✅Eligibility

  • For Ph.D. in Physics: M.Sc. in Physics. Some institutes also accept B.E./B.Tech./M.Sc./M.E./M.Tech. in relevant disciplines.
  • For Ph.D. in Theoretical Computer Science: M.Sc./M.E./M.Tech. in Computer Science and related disciplines.
  • For Integrated Ph.D.: B.Sc. in Physics or B.E./B.Tech. in relevant engineering disciplines.
  • (Note: Specific eligibility criteria can vary slightly between participating institutes.)

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age restriction** for appearing for the JEST examination.

🔬Detailed Syllabus

The syllabus is based on the general curriculum of undergraduate and postgraduate level courses.

  • Physics: Mathematical Methods, Classical Mechanics, Electromagnetism & Optics, Quantum Mechanics, Thermodynamics & Statistical Physics, and Electronics.
  • Theoretical Computer Science: Analytical Reasoning and Deduction, Combinatorics, Data Structures and Algorithms, Discrete Mathematics, Graph Theory, and Principles of Programming.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: JEST Written Examination. Candidates are shortlisted based on their rank in this single-paper, offline test.
  2. Step 2: Interviews at Participating Institutes. JEST is a screening test. Candidates must apply **separately** to each participating institute using their JEST score.
  3. Step 3: Final Selection. Each institute conducts its own interviews and/or further written tests to make the final selection. The JEST score is just one part of the overall evaluation.

💰Stipend & Fellowship (During Ph.D.)

JEST is an entrance exam for a research program, not a job. Selected candidates are admitted as Junior Research Fellows (JRF) and receive a monthly stipend as per government norms.

  • Junior Research Fellow (JRF) Stipend: Approximately ₹37,000 per month for the first two years.
  • Senior Research Fellow (SRF) Stipend: Approximately ₹42,000 per month for the remaining tenure, plus other allowances like HRA.

👨‍🔬Starting Post & Department

The starting designation upon selection is Junior Research Fellow (JRF) or Ph.D. Scholar in one of the premier research institutes of India, such as:

  • Tata Institute of Fundamental Research (TIFR)
  • Harish-Chandra Research Institute (HRI)
  • Indian Institutes of Science Education and Research (IISERs)
  • S.N. Bose National Centre for Basic Sciences
  • National Institute of Science Education and Research (NISER)

📋Profile of a Research Scholar

The role involves conducting original research under the guidance of a faculty supervisor, taking advanced coursework, publishing research papers in academic journals, and presenting work at national and international conferences. It is a career dedicated to the pursuit of knowledge and scientific discovery.

🎁Facilities After Selection (in Research Institutes)

  • Access to world-class, state-of-the-art research laboratories and equipment.
  • Interaction with some of the best scientific minds and faculty in the country.
  • Fully-funded opportunities to attend international conferences.
  • Excellent libraries, computational facilities, and on-campus accommodation.

🎯Scope

JEST is recognized as a National Eligibility Test (NET) by the Science & Engineering Research Board (SERB). It is the primary gateway for students aspiring to pursue a Ph.D. or Integrated Ph.D. in Physics and related fields at India's most prestigious research institutions. A Ph.D. from one of these institutes opens up careers in academia (as a professor), fundamental research (as a scientist in national or international labs), and R&D roles in high-tech industries.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The JEST exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically in February.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official JEST website. The process involves registration, filling the application form, uploading documents, and paying the application fee.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Parameter Details
Exam Mode Offline, Pen & Paper (OMR-based)
Number of Questions 50 (divided into 3 parts with varying marks)
Maximum Marks 100
Question Types Multiple-Choice (MCQ), Multiple Select (MSQ), and Numerical Answer Type (NAT).

Scoring Pattern: The exam has a complex structure with different marks and negative marking schemes for each of its three parts. Part A typically has 1 mark questions with -1/3 negative marking, Part B has 3 mark questions with -1 negative marking, and Part C has 3 mark questions with no negative marking.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 180 minutes (3 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 3 to 4 weeks after the examination, usually in March.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹800 for General category candidates and ₹400 for reserved categories. This fee is subject to change.
