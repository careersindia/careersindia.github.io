DETAILS ABOUT NID DAT EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

National Institute of Design - Design Aptitude Test (NID DAT).

✅Eligibility

  • For B.Des (Bachelor of Design): Candidates who have passed or are appearing for the Class XII examination in **any stream** (Science, Commerce, Arts/Humanities) from a recognized board.
  • For M.Des (Master of Design): Candidates must have completed a Bachelor's degree of a minimum of 3 years in any specialization.

🎂Age Limit (for B.Des)

Yes, there is a strict age limit for the undergraduate program. The upper age limit for General category candidates is typically **20 years** as of the admission year. Age relaxations apply for reserved categories.

🎨Detailed Syllabus

NID DAT is an aptitude test; it does not have a fixed syllabus but tests the following skills:

  • Creativity & Imagination: Ability to think of novel ideas and concepts.
  • Drawing & Sketching Skills: Proficiency in drawing, proportion, perspective, and composition.
  • Observation & Design Sensitivity: Ability to notice details and critically analyze objects and environments.
  • Analytical & Logical Reasoning: Problem-solving skills.
  • General Knowledge & Current Affairs: Awareness of social, cultural, and environmental issues.

⚙️Selection Process

The NID admission process is a rigorous two-stage examination:

  1. Stage 1: DAT Prelims. A written Design Aptitude Test that consists of both objective (MCQ) and subjective (drawing) sections.
  2. Stage 2: DAT Mains. Candidates shortlisted from Prelims are called for DAT Mains, which includes a hands-on **Studio Test** and a **Personal Interview** with a portfolio review.
  3. Final Merit List: The final list is prepared based on the combined weightage of scores from DAT Prelims and DAT Mains.

💰Salary Potential (After B.Des from NID)

NID is the most prestigious design institute in India. Placements are excellent and graduates are highly sought-after.

  • Average Salary Package: Approximately ₹18 Lakhs to ₹28 Lakhs per annum.
  • Highest Salary Package: Can go well above ₹45 Lakhs to ₹75 Lakhs per annum for top roles in major tech and product companies.

💼Starting Post & Career Paths

Graduates from NID start in influential design roles across various industries:

  • Product Designer
  • UI/UX Designer
  • Graphic Designer / Communication Designer
  • Industrial & Automotive Designer
  • Textile & Apparel Designer
  • Animator & Film Designer

🎁Facilities After Selection (at NID)

  • A world-class, creativity-focused campus environment.
  • Extensive workshops for wood, metal, ceramics, textiles, and prototyping.
  • State-of-the-art design studios and computer labs with industry-standard software.
  • A project-based curriculum with a strong emphasis on hands-on learning and research.
  • An incredibly powerful and influential alumni network in the global design community.

🎯Scope

NID DAT is the gateway to the National Institutes of Design (NID), the most revered design schools in India, established as institutions of national importance. An NID degree is considered the pinnacle of design education in the country, opening doors to leadership roles in global corporations, design studios, and successful entrepreneurial ventures. It is the "IIT of Design."

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The NID DAT is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Months: DAT Prelims is typically held in December, and DAT Mains is conducted in April/May.

📝How to Apply

The application process is conducted online through the official NID Admissions website. The process involves registration, filling the detailed application form, and uploading a portfolio (for M.Des) and other documents.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern (DAT Prelims)

Part Type Maximum Marks
Part I Objective (MCQs) 100
Part II Subjective (Drawing/Sketching)

Scoring Pattern: The objective section may have questions with varying marks. The subjective part is evaluated by a panel of experts. There is **no negative marking** in DAT Prelims.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the DAT Prelims exam is 3 hours (180 minutes).

⏳Result Time

The results for DAT Prelims are typically declared in March. The final results after DAT Mains are announced in May/June.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹3,000 for General category candidates and ₹1,500 for reserved categories. This fee is subject to change.
