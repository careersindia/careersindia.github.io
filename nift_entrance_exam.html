DETAILS ABOUT NIFT EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT) Entrance Examination.

✅Eligibility (for Undergraduate)

  • For B.Des (Bachelor of Design): Passed or appearing in the 10+2 examination from **any stream** (Science, Commerce, or Arts/Humanities).
  • For B.F.Tech (Bachelor of Fashion Technology): Passed or appearing in the 10+2 examination with **Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM)** as compulsory subjects.

🎂Age Limit (for Undergraduate)

Yes, there is an age limit. The maximum age should be less than **24 years** as of 1st August of the admission year. A 5-year relaxation is given to SC/ST/PwD candidates.

🎨Detailed Syllabus

The written exam has two parts: CAT and GAT.

  • Creative Ability Test (CAT): This is a drawing test to evaluate intuition, power of observation, innovation in design development, and design ability. It tests color and illustration skills.
  • General Ability Test (GAT):
    • Quantitative Ability: Basic math of 10th level.
    • Communication Ability & English Comprehension: Vocabulary, grammar, reading comprehension.
    • Analytical & Logical Ability: Tests inference and logic.
    • General Knowledge & Current Affairs: Awareness of recent events and general topics.
    • Case Study (for some programs): Tests situational analysis.

⚙️Selection Process (for B.Des)

The admission process is a two-stage examination:

  1. Stage 1: Written Examination. This consists of two tests held on the same day:
    • Creative Ability Test (CAT): A subjective drawing test.
    • General Ability Test (GAT): An objective multiple-choice test.
  2. Stage 2: Situation Test. Candidates shortlisted from the written exam are called for a hands-on test. They are given specific materials and asked to create a 3D model, which is evaluated on creativity, space visualization, and craftsmanship.

Note for B.F.Tech: Admission is based only on the GAT score. There is no CAT or Situation Test for B.F.Tech candidates.

💰Salary Potential (After Graduation)

Placements at NIFT are excellent, with top fashion and retail brands recruiting from campus.

  • Average Salary Package: Approximately ₹6 Lakhs to ₹12 Lakhs per annum.
  • Highest Salary Package: Can go up to ₹20 Lakhs per annum or more for top students recruited by international brands or in high-demand roles.

👗Starting Post & Career Paths

Graduates from NIFT start in various creative and managerial roles:

  • Fashion Designer / Assistant Designer
  • Fashion Merchandiser
  • Textile Designer
  • Fashion Stylist or Journalist
  • Retail Manager or Buyer
  • Production Manager (for B.F.Tech)

🎁Facilities After Selection (at NIFT)

  • State-of-the-art labs for pattern making, garment construction, weaving, and textile testing.
  • Professional design studios and computer labs with industry-standard software.
  • Resource centers with extensive libraries of books, journals, and material samples.
  • A strong industry interface for internships, graduation projects, and placements.
  • Opportunities to participate in major fashion weeks and design competitions.

🎯Scope

NIFT is the undisputed leader in fashion education in India, established under an act of the Indian Parliament. A degree from NIFT is a passport to a career in the high-growth fashion, apparel, and lifestyle industry. The institute's strong brand name and powerful alumni network provide a significant advantage for graduates throughout their careers.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The NIFT entrance exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: The written test (CAT & GAT) is typically held in February. The Situation Test for shortlisted candidates is held in April/May.

📝How to Apply

The application process is conducted online through the official NIFT admissions website. The process involves registration, filling the detailed application form, and uploading documents.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

The final merit list gives weightage to different components:

Component Weightage (for B.Des) Weightage (for B.F.Tech)
CAT (Creative Ability Test) 50% NA
GAT (General Ability Test) 30% 100%
Situation Test 20% NA

Scoring Pattern: The GAT has **negative marking** of 0.25 marks for each incorrect answer.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

  • CAT (Creative Ability Test): 180 minutes (3 hours).
  • GAT (General Ability Test): 120 minutes (2 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results of the written examination are typically declared in March. The final results after the Situation Test are announced in May/June.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹3,000 for General category candidates and ₹1,500 for reserved categories. This fee is subject to change.
