DETAILS ABOUT RRB-JE EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Railway Recruitment Board - Junior Engineer (RRB JE).

✅Eligibility

  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India (or other eligible categories as per Govt. norms).
  • Academic Qualification: Must hold a three-year Diploma or a Bachelor's Degree (B.E./B.Tech) in the relevant engineering discipline from a recognized institution.

🎂Age Limit

The candidate must be between **18 and 33 years** of age. Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/Ex-Servicemen) as per government rules.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • CBT-1 (Common for all): Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness, and General Science.
  • CBT-2:
    • Part A (Common): General Awareness, Physics & Chemistry, Basics of Computers and Applications, Basics of Environment and Pollution Control.
    • Part B (Technical): Syllabus is specific to the chosen engineering discipline (e.g., Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, etc.) at a Diploma/Degree level.

⚙️Selection Process

The selection is a four-stage process:

  1. Step 1: First Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-1). This is a screening test that is qualifying in nature.
  2. Step 2: Second Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-2). Marks from this stage are used for the final merit list.
  3. Step 3: Document Verification. Shortlisted candidates are called for verification of their original documents.
  4. Step 4: Medical Examination. A medical fitness test is conducted to ensure the candidate meets the medical standards required for the post.

💰Starting Salary Potential

An RRB Junior Engineer is a Group 'C' post in Level 6 of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix. The in-hand starting salary, including all allowances in a metropolitan city, is approximately ₹55,000 - ₹65,000 per month.

🚂Starting Post & Department

The starting post is **Junior Engineer (JE)** in various technical departments of the Indian Railways, such as:

  • JE (Works / Civil)
  • JE (P-Way - Permanent Way/Track)
  • JE (Mechanical / Electrical)
  • JE (Signal & Telecommunication)
  • JE (IT)

📋Job Profile

The job of a Junior Engineer in the Railways is a technical supervisory role. It involves supervising maintenance and construction work, ensuring the safety and quality of railway assets (like tracks, signals, coaches), managing technical staff, and preparing reports and estimates.

🎁Facilities After Selection

  • A highly secure and stable central government job.
  • Pension benefits under the National Pension System (NPS).
  • Comprehensive medical facilities for self and family (RELHS).
  • **Privilege Passes / PTOs:** Free and subsidized train travel for self and family across India, a unique and major perk of a railway job.
  • Railway quarters for accommodation at a very low cost.

🎯Scope

A career as a Junior Engineer in Indian Railways offers excellent growth and stability. A JE can be promoted to **Senior Section Engineer (SSE)**. Through further departmental exams, one can rise to the level of Assistant Divisional Engineer (ADEN), which is a Group 'A' Gazetted Officer post, and even higher in the railway hierarchy.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The RRB-JE exam is **not conducted annually**. It is held based on the large-scale vacancy requirements of the Indian Railways, often with a gap of 2-4 years between notifications.
  • Exam Months: The exam schedule varies greatly depending on the recruitment cycle.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official websites of the respective regional Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) during the application window.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

  • CBT-1: 90 minutes (1.5 hours).
  • CBT-2: 120 minutes (2 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results for each stage are typically declared within 1 to 2 months after the examination.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically ₹500 for General/OBC male candidates (with ₹400 refunded after appearing in CBT-1) and ₹250 for female candidates and reserved categories (fully refunded after appearing).

🌐Official Website

For all official notifications, candidates must check the websites of the regional RRBs or the main portal of the Indian Railways: indianrailways.gov.in
