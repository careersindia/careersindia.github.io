ℹ️Full Form

Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) Recruitment Examination.

✅Eligibility (for UDC Post)

  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India.
  • Academic Qualification: A degree from a recognized University or equivalent.
  • Computer Skills: Must have a working knowledge of computers including the use of Office Suites and databases.

🎂Age Limit (for UDC Post)

The candidate must be between **18 and 27 years** of age. Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/PwD) as per government norms.

📖Detailed Syllabus (for UDC Post)

  • General Intelligence and Reasoning: Analogy, Classification, Series, Coding-Decoding, Blood Relations, Syllogism, and other verbal/non-verbal reasoning topics.
  • General Awareness: Current events, Indian History, Geography, Economy, Polity, and General Science. Special emphasis on insurance-related topics.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Number System, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Ratio, Time & Work, Time & Distance, Data Interpretation.
  • English Comprehension: Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Spotting Errors, Vocabulary (Synonyms/Antonyms), Grammar.

⚙️Selection Process (for UDC Post)

The selection is conducted in three phases:

  1. Phase-I: Preliminary Examination. An online objective test that is qualifying in nature.
  2. Phase-II: Main Examination. An online objective test. Marks from this phase are considered for the final merit list.
  3. Phase-III: Computer Skill Test. This is a qualifying test to check proficiency in PowerPoint, MS Word, and Excel.

💰Starting Salary Potential (for UDC Post)

ESIC UDC is a Group 'C' post in Pay Level-4. The in-hand starting salary, including allowances in a metropolitan city, is approximately ₹40,000 - ₹45,000 per month.

🏢Starting Post & Department

The starting post is **Upper Division Clerk (UDC)** in the Employees' State Insurance Corporation, which is an autonomous body under the Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India.

📋Job Profile

The job of a UDC is primarily clerical and administrative. Responsibilities include:

  • Handling official correspondence and drafting notes.
  • Maintaining files and records.
  • Processing insurance claims and disbursing benefits.
  • Online data entry and account keeping.

🎁Facilities After Selection

  • A secure government-backed job with a stable career.
  • Pension benefits under the National Pension System (NPS).
  • Excellent medical benefits for self and family through ESIC's own network of hospitals and dispensaries.
  • Paid leaves, Leave Travel Concession (LTC), and other standard government allowances.

🎯Scope

A job as an ESIC UDC offers significant career growth. Through departmental exams, a UDC can get promoted to Assistant and then to Social Security Officer (SSO), which is a gazetted officer-level post. It provides a secure pathway to a managerial role within the organization.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The ESIC recruitment exams are **not conducted annually**. They are held based on the vacancies and requirements of the corporation, often with a gap of 2-3 years between notifications.
  • Exam Months: The exam schedule varies greatly depending on the recruitment cycle.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the "Recruitment" section of the official ESIC website. The process involves registration, form filling, document upload, and fee payment.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹500 for General/OBC male candidates and ₹250 for women candidates and reserved categories (this amount is often refunded after appearing in the exam for the latter). This fee is subject to change.
