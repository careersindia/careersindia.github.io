DETAILS ABOUT CDS EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Combined Defence Services (CDS) Examination.

✅Eligibility

  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India (or subject of Nepal/Bhutan, or Tibetan refugee).
  • Marital Status: Unmarried.
  • For IMA & OTA: Degree from a recognized university or equivalent.
  • For Indian Naval Academy: Degree in Engineering from a recognized university.
  • For Air Force Academy: Degree of a recognized university (with Physics and Mathematics at 10+2 level) or Bachelor of Engineering.

🎂Age Limit

  • For IMA & INA: 19 to 24 years.
  • For Air Force Academy: 20 to 24 years.
  • For Officers' Training Academy (OTA): 19 to 25 years.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • English: Vocabulary, Grammar, Reading Comprehension, Error Spotting, Sentence Improvement, and Jumbled Sentences (Para Jumbles).
  • General Knowledge: Current Affairs (National & International), History of India (especially Freedom Struggle), Geography, Indian Polity, Basic Economics, and General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology of Class 10th level).
  • Elementary Mathematics (Only for IMA, INA, AFA): Arithmetic, Algebra, Trigonometry, Geometry, Mensuration, and Statistics of Class 10th level.

⚙️Selection Process

The selection is a two-stage process:

  1. Step 1: Written Examination. Candidates who qualify in the written exam are shortlisted for the next stage.
  2. Step 2: SSB Interview. The Service Selection Board (SSB) conducts a comprehensive 5-day interview process which includes psychological tests, group tasks, and a personal interview to assess officer-like qualities.
  3. Step 3: Medical Examination. Candidates recommended by the SSB undergo a detailed medical examination.
  4. Step 4: Final Merit List. A merit list is prepared based on the total marks in the written exam and the SSB interview.

💰Starting Salary Potential

After successful training and commissioning, an officer starts as a Lieutenant (in Army), Sub-Lieutenant (in Navy), or Flying Officer (in Air Force) in Pay Level 10. The starting salary, including all allowances, is approximately ₹85,000 - ₹1,00,000 per month.

🎖️Starting Post & Department

The starting rank and branch depend on the academy joined:

  • Indian Military Academy (IMA): Lieutenant in the Indian Army (Infantry, Artillery, Armoured Corps, etc.).
  • Indian Naval Academy (INA): Sub-Lieutenant in the Indian Navy (Executive, Technical, etc.).
  • Air Force Academy (AFA): Flying Officer in the Indian Air Force (Flying, Technical, or Ground Duty).
  • Officers' Training Academy (OTA): Lieutenant in the Indian Army (Short Service Commission).

📋Job Profile

The job profile of an officer in the armed forces is one of leadership and command. It involves leading troops, managing strategic assets, and participating in operations. It is a highly respected and dynamic career that demands physical fitness, mental alertness, and unwavering integrity.

🎁Facilities After Selection

Officers are entitled to a wide range of facilities and perks, including:

  • Free accommodation and medical facilities for self and family.
  • Access to Canteen Store Department (CSD) facilities.
  • Generous leave policies (60 days annual leave, 20 days casual leave).
  • Travel concessions and access to officers' clubs and messes.
  • Opportunities for higher education and foreign postings.

🎯Scope

A career in the armed forces offers immense scope for personal and professional growth. There is a well-defined career progression path with opportunities to rise to the highest ranks. It provides a life of honor, adventure, and the unparalleled opportunity to serve the nation.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The CDS exam is conducted twice a year by UPSC.
  • Exam Months: CDS (I) is typically held in April, and CDS (II) is held in September.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official UPSC website. The process involves a two-part registration, filling the application form, uploading documents, and paying the fee.
