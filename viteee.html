DETAILS ABOUT VITEEE EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Vellore Institute of Technology Engineering Entrance Examination (VITEEE).

✅Eligibility

  • Nationality: Resident Indian, NRI, PIO, or OCI cardholder.
  • Academic Qualification: Must have passed or be appearing in the 10+2 (or equivalent) examination.
  • Subjects & Marks: A minimum of 55% aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics/Biology (PCM/PCB) for General category (45% for reserved categories).

🎂Age Limit

Yes, there is an age limit. Candidates must have a date of birth on or after July 1st of the year they are taking the exam (e.g., July 1, 2002 for VITEEE 2024).

📖Detailed Syllabus

The syllabus is based on the State Board of Higher Secondary Education and the CBSE curriculum for Class XI & XII.

  • Physics: Laws of Motion, Work, Energy & Power, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects, Optics, Modern Physics.
  • Chemistry: Atomic Structure, Thermodynamics, Electrochemistry, Organic & Inorganic Chemistry.
  • Mathematics: Algebra, Calculus, Trigonometry, Coordinate Geometry, Vectors, Probability.
  • Biology (for B.Tech Biotechnology, etc.): Cell Biology, Genetics, Plant & Human Physiology, Ecology.
  • Aptitude & English: Data Interpretation, Data Sufficiency, Syllogism, Number Series, and basic English grammar & comprehension.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Appear for VITEEE. Candidates appear for the Computer-Based Test (CBT).
  2. Step 2: Rank Declaration. A merit list is prepared based on the candidate's percentile score, and an All India Rank is assigned.
  3. Step 3: Online Counselling. Candidates with a valid rank participate in online counselling where they fill their choices of campus (Vellore, Chennai, AP, Bhopal) and engineering branch. Seats are allotted based on rank and availability.

💰Placement & Salary Potential (After B.Tech)

VIT is renowned for its massive placement drives, attracting a very large number of national and international companies.

  • Average Salary Package: Approximately ₹9.23 Lakhs per annum.
  • Highest Salary Package: Frequently exceeds ₹1 Crore per annum for international offers.

🎁Facilities After Selection (at VIT)

  • Large, modern campuses with international-standard infrastructure.
  • Fully Flexible Credit System (FFCS™): A unique system allowing students to choose their own subjects, professors, and class timings.
  • Strong emphasis on research, with numerous labs and innovation centers.
  • Opportunities for international exposure through exchange programs.
  • Excellent hostel facilities and a vibrant, multicultural campus life.

🎯Scope

VITEEE is the entrance exam for the Vellore Institute of Technology, an "Institution of Eminence" and one of the most sought-after private engineering universities in India. A degree from VIT provides excellent career opportunities, a strong academic foundation, and a globally recognized qualification. VIT's massive placement record makes it a top choice for students aiming for a successful corporate career.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The VITEEE is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Months: The exam is typically held over a window of several days in April.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official VIT admissions website. The process involves registration, filling the application form, uploading documents, and paying the application fee.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Subject Number of Questions
Mathematics / Biology 40
Physics 35
Chemistry 35
Aptitude 10
English 5
Total 125

Scoring Pattern: +1 mark for every correct answer. There is **no negative marking** for incorrect answers.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 150 minutes (2 hours and 30 minutes).

⏳Result Time

The results are declared very quickly, usually within 10 to 15 days after the conclusion of the exam window.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹1,350. This fee is subject to change.
