DETAILS ABOUT FCI EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Food Corporation of India (FCI) Recruitment Examination.

✅Eligibility

Eligibility varies by post. For the most common post, Assistant Grade III (AG-III):

  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India.
  • Educational Qualification:
    • AG-III (General): Graduate Degree in any discipline.
    • AG-III (Accounts): Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com).
    • AG-III (Technical): B.Sc. in Agriculture or B.Sc. with Botany/Zoology/Bio-Technology/Bio-Chemistry/Microbiology/Food Science.

🎂Age Limit

The age limit also varies by post. For Assistant Grade III, the maximum age limit is typically **27 years**. Age relaxations apply for reserved categories as per government norms.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The syllabus is similar to major banking and SSC exams.

  • English Language: Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Para Jumbles, Error Spotting, Vocabulary.
  • Reasoning Ability: Puzzles, Seating Arrangement, Syllogism, Blood Relation, Coding-Decoding, Inequality.
  • Numerical Aptitude: Data Interpretation (DI), Simplification, Number Series, Quadratic Equations, and Arithmetic problems.
  • General Studies: Indian History, Geography, Economy, General Science (up to Class 8), and Current Affairs. For technical posts, specific subject knowledge is tested in Phase-II.

⚙️Selection Process

The selection is typically conducted in two phases for AG-III posts:

  1. Phase-I: Online Preliminary Exam. An objective test that is qualifying in nature.
  2. Phase-II: Online Main Exam. Consists of one or two papers depending on the post. Marks from this phase are considered for the final merit list.
  3. Step 3: Document Verification. Shortlisted candidates are called for document verification.

💰Starting Salary Potential

The salary for an FCI Assistant Grade III varies based on the city of posting. The in-hand starting salary in a metropolitan city is approximately ₹40,000 - ₹50,000 per month.

🏢Starting Post & Department

The starting post is **Assistant Grade III** in one of the functional cadres:

  • General (General Administration)
  • Accounts (Finance and Accounts)
  • Technical (Quality Control)
  • Depot (Storage and Movement of Foodgrains)

📋Job Profile

  • AG-III (General): Primarily administrative work, including maintaining files, correspondence, and assisting in the general functioning of the office.
  • AG-III (Accounts): Maintaining account books, processing payments, and handling financial records.
  • AG-III (Technical): Ensuring the quality of procured and stored foodgrains through scientific checks.
  • AG-III (Depot): Overseeing the loading, unloading, and storage operations of foodgrains at depots.

🎁Facilities After Selection

  • Job security in a major Public Sector Undertaking.
  • Defined pay scales with allowances like HRA, DA, etc.
  • Pension benefits under the EPF scheme.
  • Medical facilities for self and dependents.
  • Gratuity and Leave Travel Concession (LTC).

🎯Scope

A career in FCI offers excellent stability and plays a crucial role in India's food security system. There is a well-structured career progression path. An Assistant Grade III can be promoted to higher grades (AG-II, AG-I) and eventually to the managerial cadre (Manager, AGM, DGM) based on performance and departmental exams.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The FCI exam is **not conducted annually**. It is held based on the vacancies and requirements of the corporation, often with a gap of a few years between major recruitments.
  • Exam Months: The notification and exam dates vary significantly.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official FCI recruitment website during the specified application window. The process involves registration, form filling, document upload, and fee payment.

⏳Result Time

The results for each phase are typically declared within 1 to 2 months after the examination.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹500 for General/OBC male candidates. **Women candidates and candidates belonging to SC, ST, PwD, and Ex-servicemen categories are exempted from fee payment.**
