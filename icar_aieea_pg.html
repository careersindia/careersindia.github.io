ℹ️Full Form

Indian Council of Agricultural Research - All India Entrance Examination for Admission (Postgraduate).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: Candidates must have a Bachelor's degree in a relevant field of agriculture or allied sciences. The degree must follow the 10+2+4 years or similar structure as specified by ICAR.
  • Marks: A minimum of 60% aggregate marks for General/OBC/EWS category (50% for SC/ST/PwD candidates).

🎂Age Limit

The candidate must be at least **19 years** of age. There is no upper age limit.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The syllabus is **subject-specific** and based on the undergraduate curriculum of that discipline. Candidates choose one major subject group for the exam from a list that includes:

  • Plant Sciences, Physical Science, Entomology and Nematology, Agronomy, Social Sciences, Horticulture, etc.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Appear for ICAR AIEEA-PG Exam. This is a single-session Computer-Based Test (CBT).
  2. Step 2: Rank Card Release. The NTA releases a subject-wise rank card based on the percentile score.
  3. Step 3: Centralized Online Counselling. Candidates with a valid rank participate in online counselling for seat allotment to M.Sc./M.Tech programs in various Agricultural Universities.

💰Stipend & Salary Potential

During M.Sc. program: Students admitted through ICAR are eligible for the **Post Graduate Scholarship (PGS)**, which provides a monthly stipend of approximately ₹12,640 per month.

After M.Sc. (Post-Graduation):

  • Government Sector (Scientist after ARS/NET): Starting salary of ₹80,000 - ₹95,000 per month.
  • Private Sector (e.g., R&D roles): Starting salary of ₹6 Lakhs to ₹12 Lakhs per annum.

🌱Starting Post & Career Paths

After completing an M.Sc. in Agriculture or allied sciences, career paths become more specialized:

  • Scientist in ICAR institutions after qualifying the ARS exam.
  • Assistant Professor in Agricultural Universities after qualifying NET.
  • Subject Matter Specialist (SMS) in Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs).
  • Research and Development (R&D) Officer in private seed, fertilizer, and agri-tech companies.
  • Pursuing a Ph.D. for a career in advanced research and academia.

🎁Facilities After Selection (in Agricultural Universities)

  • Advanced research laboratories and experimental farms.
  • Guidance from senior scientists and experienced faculty for thesis research.
  • Access to specialized libraries and digital databases.
  • Opportunities to participate in national seminars and workshops.

🎯Scope

ICAR-AIEEA-PG is the premier national-level entrance exam for admission to Master's degree programs in agriculture and allied sciences. It is the gateway for the 25% All India Quota seats in top State Agricultural Universities (SAUs) and 100% seats in ICAR Deemed Universities. A Master's degree is the essential qualification for a career as a scientist, researcher, or academician in the agricultural sector and makes one eligible for the prestigious Agricultural Research Service (ARS) exam.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically in June or July.

📝How to Apply

The application process is managed by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Candidates must apply online through the official NTA ICAR website.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Parameter Details
Exam Mode Computer-Based Test (CBT)
Number of Questions 120 (Multiple Choice Questions)
Question Distribution Questions from the major subject group and sub-subjects.
Maximum Marks 480

Scoring Pattern: +4 marks for every correct answer, -1 mark for every incorrect answer.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 120 minutes (2 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 3 to 4 weeks after the examination date.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹1,200 for General category candidates, with lower fees for reserved categories. This fee is subject to change.
