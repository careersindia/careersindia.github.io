DETAILS ABOUT MAT EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Management Aptitude Test (MAT).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: A candidate must be a graduate in any discipline from a recognized university.
  • Final Year Students: Candidates appearing for the final year of their Bachelor’s degree are also eligible to apply.
  • Marks Criteria: There is no minimum percentage requirement to appear for the MAT exam itself, but individual participating colleges have their own criteria (usually 50%).

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for candidates to appear for the MAT exam.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • Language Comprehension: Reading Comprehension, Vocabulary (Synonyms, Antonyms), Grammar, Para Jumbles, Sentence Correction.
  • Intelligence & Critical Reasoning: Analytical Reasoning, Puzzles, Blood Relations, Direction Sense, Series, Statements & Assumptions/Arguments.
  • Mathematical Skills: Arithmetic (Percentage, Profit & Loss, etc.), Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, and Modern Math.
  • Data Analysis & Sufficiency: Interpretation of Tables, Graphs (Line, Bar, Pie), Charts, and Data Sufficiency questions.
  • Indian & Global Environment: General Knowledge, Current Affairs (National & International), Business & Economy, Corporate News, and Static GK.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Appear for the MAT Exam. Candidates appear for the test in their chosen mode (PBT, CBT, or IBT).
  2. Step 2: Scorecard Release. AIMA releases a scorecard containing the candidate's sectional and overall scaled scores and percentile.
  3. Step 3: Apply to B-Schools. Candidates use their MAT scorecard to apply individually to the hundreds of B-schools that accept MAT scores.
  4. Step 4: College Selection Process. Each participating institute conducts its own selection process, which typically includes Group Discussion (GD), a Written Ability Test (WAT), and/or a Personal Interview (PI).

💰Starting Salary Potential (After MBA)

Salaries depend heavily on the college a candidate gets into. MAT scores are accepted by a wide range of Tier-2 and Tier-3 B-schools.

  • Average Salary Package: Approximately ₹6 Lakhs to ₹12 Lakhs per annum in good colleges.
  • Highest Salary Package: Can go up to ₹18 Lakhs to ₹25 Lakhs per annum in some of the top colleges accepting MAT scores.

🎁Facilities After Selection (in B-Schools)

  • Industry-focused curriculum and experienced faculty.
  • Strong placement cells with corporate connections.
  • Opportunities for internships and practical industry exposure.
  • Development of managerial and leadership skills.

🎯Scope

MAT is a national-level MBA entrance exam conducted by the All India Management Association (AIMA). Its score is accepted by over 600 business schools across India. Due to its high frequency and moderate difficulty level, it is a very popular and accessible option for students seeking admission to a wide range of MBA and PGDM programs.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: MAT is conducted **multiple times a year**, typically in four main phases.
  • Exam Months: The exam sessions are usually held in **February, May, September, and December**.
  • Exam Modes: It offers flexibility with three testing modes: Paper-Based Test (PBT), Computer-Based Test (CBT), and Internet-Based Test (IBT).

📝How to Apply

Candidates must register and apply online through the official AIMA MAT website. During registration, they can select their preferred testing mode and test city.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Section Number of Questions Suggested Time (Mins)
Language Comprehension 40 30
Intelligence & Critical Reasoning 40 30
Mathematical Skills 40 40
Data Analysis & Sufficiency 40 35
Indian & Global Environment* 40 15
Total 200 150 Minutes

Scoring Pattern: +1 mark for every correct answer, -0.25 marks for every incorrect answer.

*The score of the "Indian & Global Environment" section is reported separately and is usually not included in the calculation of the final composite score and percentile.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 150 minutes (2 hours and 30 minutes) with no strict sectional time limits, but suggested times are provided.

⏳Result Time

The results for each session are typically declared within **3 to 4 weeks** after the examination.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹2,100 for one test mode or ₹3,300 for two test modes (e.g., CBT and PBT). This fee is subject to change.
