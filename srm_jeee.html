ℹ️Full Form

SRM Joint Engineering Entrance Examination (SRMJEEE).

✅Eligibility

  • Nationality: Resident Indian, Non-Resident Indian (NRI), holder of PIO or OCI card issued by the Government of India are eligible.
  • Academic Qualification: Passed or appearing in 10+2 (or equivalent) examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM) as major subjects.
  • Marks: Minimum aggregate of 60% in PCM for the main Kattankulathur campus, and 50% for other campuses.

🎂Age Limit

The candidate must have completed 16 years of age as on 31st July of the admission year. There is no upper age limit.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The syllabus is based on the Class XI & XII curriculum.

  • Physics: Mechanics, Optics, Electricity & Magnetism, Thermodynamics, Modern Physics.
  • Chemistry: Atomic Structure, Chemical Bonding, States of Matter, Electrochemistry, Organic & Inorganic Chemistry.
  • Mathematics: Algebra, Calculus, Trigonometry, Coordinate Geometry, Vectors, Probability.
  • English: Reading comprehension passages and basic grammar questions.
  • Aptitude: Number systems, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Direction sense, Puzzles.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Appear for SRMJEEE Online Test. Candidates appear for the remote proctored online test in their chosen phase.
  2. Step 2: Rank List Declaration. A rank list is prepared based on the candidate's percentile score in the exam.
  3. Step 3: Online Counselling. Shortlisted candidates participate in online counselling for choice filling and seat allotment in one of the SRM campuses (Kattankulathur, Ramapuram, Vadapalani, Ghaziabad, Sonepat).

💰Placement & Salary Potential (After B.Tech)

SRM is one of the top private universities in India with an excellent placement record, attracting a vast number of recruiters.

  • Average Salary Package: Approximately ₹7.5 Lakhs to ₹10 Lakhs per annum.
  • Highest Salary Package: Frequently exceeds ₹1 Crore per annum for top students securing "super dream" and international offers.

🎯Scope

SRMJEEE is one of the most popular private university engineering entrance exams in India. It is the gateway to the highly-ranked SRM Institute of Science and Technology. A degree from SRM provides a strong platform for a successful career in engineering and technology, with a reputation for excellent placements and a global alumni network.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The SRMJEEE is conducted in **multiple phases** (typically Phase 1, 2, and 3) each year.
  • Exam Months: The phases are spread out between **April and July**.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official SRMIST admissions website. The process involves filling the application form, uploading documents, and paying the application fee.
