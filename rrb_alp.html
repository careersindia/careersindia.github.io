ℹ️Full Form

Railway Recruitment Board - Assistant Loco Pilot (RRB ALP).

✅Eligibility

  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India (or other eligible categories as per Govt. norms).
  • Academic Qualification: Matriculation (10th Class) pass plus an ITI certificate in specified trades (e.g., Fitter, Electrician, Millwright) OR a 3-year Diploma in Mechanical / Electrical / Electronics / Automobile Engineering. B.E./B.Tech in these streams is also acceptable.

🎂Age Limit

The candidate must be between **18 and 30 years** of age. Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/Ex-Servicemen) as per government rules.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • CBT-1: Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Science, and General Awareness on Current Affairs.
  • CBT-2 (Part A): Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, and Basic Science & Engineering.
  • CBT-2 (Part B): This is a qualifying paper on the relevant trade syllabus as prescribed by the DGET.

⚙️Selection Process

The selection is a multi-stage process:

  1. Step 1: First Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-1). A screening exam to shortlist candidates for the next stage.
  2. Step 2: Second Stage Computer Based Test (CBT-2). This has two parts: Part A (for merit) and Part B (qualifying).
  3. Step 3: Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT). This is a mandatory psycho-aptitude test for ALP candidates who clear CBT-2. It is also qualifying in nature.
  4. Step 4: Document Verification and Medical Examination. The final stage before appointment.

💰Starting Salary Potential

An RRB ALP is in Level 2 of the 7th CPC Pay Matrix. The initial in-hand salary is around ₹35,000 - ₹45,000 per month. However, this increases significantly with "Running Allowances" (like Kilometer Allowance) once posted on active train duties.

🚂Starting Post & Department

The starting post is **Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP)** in the Electrical or Mechanical Department of the Indian Railways.

📋Job Profile

The job of an Assistant Loco Pilot is a highly responsible operational role. Key responsibilities include:

  • Assisting the main Loco Pilot in running the train.
  • Continuously monitoring the train's control panel and signals.
  • Checking the locomotive's condition before starting a trip.
  • Attending to minor repairs in the locomotive if needed.
  • Calling out signals to the main pilot.

🎁Facilities After Selection

  • A secure central government job with immense responsibility.
  • Pension benefits under the National Pension System (NPS).
  • Comprehensive railway medical facilities.
  • **Privilege Passes / PTOs:** Free and subsidized train travel for self and family.
  • Railway quarters for accommodation.

🎯Scope

A career as an Assistant Loco Pilot has a very clear and structured growth path. It is the first step to becoming a Loco Pilot, who is the in-charge of running a train. The promotion hierarchy is: ALP → Senior ALP → Loco Pilot (Shunting) → Loco Pilot (Goods) → Loco Pilot (Passenger) → Loco Pilot (Mail/Express). It is a challenging but rewarding career at the heart of railway operations.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The RRB-ALP exam is **not conducted annually**. It is held based on large-scale vacancy requirements, often with a gap of several years between notifications.
  • Exam Months: The exam schedule varies greatly depending on the recruitment cycle.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official websites of the respective regional Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) during the application window.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

First Stage CBT (CBT-1): 75 questions in 60 minutes.

Final Merit List Calculation: 70% weightage to marks from CBT-2 Part A and 30% weightage to marks from the Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT).

Scoring Pattern: There is **negative marking** of 1/3rd of the marks for each incorrect answer in both CBT-1 and CBT-2.

⏳Result Time

The results for each stage are typically declared within 1 to 2 months after the examination.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically ₹500 for General/OBC male candidates (with ₹400 refunded after appearing in CBT-1) and ₹250 for female candidates and reserved categories (fully refunded after appearing).
