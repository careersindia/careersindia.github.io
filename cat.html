DETAILS ABOUT CAT EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Common Admission Test (CAT).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: Must hold a Bachelor's Degree with at least 50% marks or equivalent CGPA (45% for SC, ST, and PwD categories).
  • Final Year Students: Candidates appearing for the final year of their Bachelor’s degree are also eligible to apply.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for appearing in the CAT exam.

📖Detailed Syllabus

CAT does not have a fixed syllabus but tests aptitude in the following areas:

  • Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC): Reading Comprehension passages, Para Jumbles, Para Summary, and Out of Context Sentences.
  • Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (DILR): Caselets and sets based on tables, graphs, charts (bar, pie), and logical reasoning puzzles involving arrangement, grouping, and conditions.
  • Quantitative Ability (QA): Arithmetic (Percentages, Profit & Loss, Time & Work), Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Number System, and Modern Math (Permutation & Combination, Probability).

⚙️Selection Process

The selection process for the IIMs and other top B-schools is a two-stage process:

  1. Step 1: The CAT Exam. Candidates are first shortlisted based on their CAT percentile and academic profile.
  2. Step 2: WAT/GD-PI Round. Shortlisted candidates are called for the second stage, which typically includes:
    • Written Ability Test (WAT) or Essay.
    • Group Discussion (GD).
    • Personal Interview (PI).

The final merit list is prepared based on a composite score including CAT score, WAT/GD-PI performance, and academic background.

💰Salary Potential (After MBA)

Graduates from top IIMs (Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Calcutta, etc.) command some of the highest salaries in the country.

  • Average Salary Package: Approximately ₹30 Lakhs to ₹35 Lakhs per annum.
  • Highest Salary Package: Can exceed ₹1 Crore to ₹1.5 Crores per annum, including international offers.

🚀Starting Post & Department (After MBA)

An MBA from a top IIM is a launchpad for leadership roles. Common starting posts include:

  • Management Consultant at firms like McKinsey, BCG, Bain & Company.
  • Product Manager at leading tech companies like Google, Microsoft, Amazon.
  • Investment Banking Analyst at firms like Goldman Sachs, JP Morgan.
  • Brand Manager at major FMCG companies like P&G, Unilever.
  • General Management Roles in leadership tracks of large conglomerates.

📋Job Profile

Post-MBA roles are strategic and high-impact. The job profile involves solving complex business problems, managing teams and large-scale projects, formulating marketing strategies, handling corporate finance, and driving business growth. It is a fast-track to managerial and leadership positions.

🎁Facilities After Selection (in an IIM)

  • A highly competitive and intellectually stimulating environment.
  • World-renowned faculty and a case-study based pedagogy.
  • Opportunities for international student exchange programs with top global business schools.
  • A powerful and extremely well-connected global alumni network.
  • Excellent infrastructure for academics, sports, and housing.

🎯Scope

The CAT is the most prestigious entrance exam for admission to the Master of Business Administration (MBA) and other postgraduate management programs in India. A high score opens the doors to the 21 Indian Institutes of Management (IIMs) and over 1,200 other top business schools. An MBA from a premier institute provides unparalleled career acceleration, leadership opportunities, and a pathway to global business roles.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The CAT exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically on the last Sunday of November.

📝How to Apply

The application process is conducted online through the official IIMCAT website. It involves registration, filling a detailed application form with academic and personal details, selecting IIM programs, and paying the application fee.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Section Number of Questions Sectional Time Limit
Verbal Ability & Reading Comprehension (VARC) 24 40 Minutes
Data Interpretation & Logical Reasoning (DILR) 20 40 Minutes
Quantitative Ability (QA) 22 40 Minutes
Total 66 120 Minutes

Scoring Pattern: +3 marks for every correct answer, -1 mark for every incorrect MCQ. There is **no negative marking** for Non-MCQ (Type In The Answer - TITA) questions.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 120 minutes (2 hours), with a strict sectional time limit of 40 minutes for each section.

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared in the first or second week of January, approximately 40 days after the exam.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹2,400 for General/OBC candidates and ₹1,200 for SC/ST/PwD candidates. This fee is subject to change.
