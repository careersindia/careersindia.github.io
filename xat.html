DETAILS ABOUT XAT EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Xavier Aptitude Test (XAT).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: Must hold a Bachelor's Degree of a minimum of 3 years duration in any discipline from a recognized university.
  • Final Year Students: Candidates appearing for the final year of their Bachelor’s degree are also eligible to apply.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for candidates to appear for the XAT exam.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • Verbal and Logical Ability: Reading Comprehension, Critical Reasoning, Grammar, Vocabulary, Para Jumbles.
  • Decision Making: This is a unique and critical section. It involves questions based on case studies, situational puzzles, and ethical dilemmas that test managerial and decision-making skills.
  • Quantitative Ability & Data Interpretation: Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Modern Math, and Data Interpretation sets (tables, graphs, charts).
  • General Knowledge: Static GK (History, Geography, Science, Books & Authors) and Current Affairs (National & International, Business, Economy).
  • Analytical Essay Writing: A short essay on a given topic to test writing skills and coherence of thought.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: The XAT Exam. Candidates appear for the single-session computer-based test.
  2. Step 2: Scorecard and Shortlisting. Candidates are shortlisted by XLRI and other participating B-schools based on their XAT sectional and overall scores.
  3. Step 3: GD/PI Round. Shortlisted candidates are called for the second stage, which typically includes a Group Discussion (GD) and multiple rounds of Personal Interviews (PI).

The final merit list is prepared based on a composite score including XAT score, GD/PI performance, and academic profile.

💰Salary Potential (After MBA from XLRI/Top Colleges)

Placements at XLRI are on par with the top IIMs. Other top colleges accepting XAT scores (like XIMB, IMT-G) also offer excellent packages.

  • Average Salary Package (XLRI): Approximately ₹30 Lakhs to ₹35 Lakhs per annum.
  • Highest Salary Package (XLRI): Frequently exceeds ₹1 Crore per annum for roles in consulting and finance.

NA (This is an entrance exam for a postgraduate course). Graduates secure top roles like Management Consultant, HR Manager (XLRI is famous for HR), Product Manager, etc.

🎁Facilities After Selection (in XLRI/Top B-Schools)

  • A rigorous, case-based learning environment with world-renowned faculty.
  • A strong focus on business ethics and social responsibility.
  • An exceptionally powerful and well-connected alumni network, especially in HR and corporate leadership.
  • Excellent placement opportunities with top global recruiters.

🎯Scope

XAT is one of the oldest and most prestigious MBA entrance exams in India, conducted by XLRI Jamshedpur. A high XAT score is the primary gateway to XLRI, one of India's elite B-schools, and is also accepted by over 160 other top management institutes like XIMB, IMT Ghaziabad, and TAPMI. It is highly regarded for its unique Decision Making section, which tests managerial aptitude effectively.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The XAT exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Traditionally on the first Sunday of January.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must register and apply online through the official XAT website. During the XAT application, they can also select and pay for the programs at XLRI. For other participating institutes, candidates need to apply separately using their XAT ID.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 210 minutes (3 hours and 30 minutes).

⏳Result Time

The results are declared very quickly, typically in the last week of January, within 3 weeks of the exam.

💸Fees

The application fee for the XAT exam is typically around ₹2,100. There is an additional fee of ~₹200 for each XLRI program a candidate wishes to apply to.
