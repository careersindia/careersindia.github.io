ℹ️Full Form

National Entrance Screening Test (NEST).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: Candidates must have passed the Class 12 examination (or equivalent) from the Science stream.
  • Marks: Must have secured a minimum of 60% aggregate marks in Class 12 (55% for SC, ST, and PwD candidates).
  • Year of Appearance: Must have passed the Class 12 examination in the current year or the two preceding years.

🎂Age Limit

Yes, there is an age limit. Candidates from the General and OBC categories should not have been born earlier than a specified date (e.g., August 1, 2004 for NEST 2024). Age relaxation of 5 years is given to SC, ST, and PwD candidates.

🔬Detailed Syllabus

The syllabus is based on the CBSE curriculum for Class XI and XII, but questions are designed to test comprehension and analytical ability.

  • General Section: This section is compulsory and does not have a specific syllabus. It tests general scientific aptitude, historical awareness of science, and logical reasoning.
  • Biology: Cell Biology, Genetics and Evolution, Ecology, Humans and Environment, Biotechnology, Biology of Animal and Plant Systems.
  • Chemistry: Physical, Organic, and Inorganic Chemistry topics from the Class XI-XII syllabus.
  • Mathematics: Algebra, Trigonometry, Analytical Geometry, Differential & Integral Calculus, Vectors, and Matrices.
  • Physics: Mechanics, Thermal Physics, Electricity & Magnetism, Optics, and Modern Physics.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: NEST Written Examination. A single-session, computer-based test (CBT).
  2. Step 2: Merit List Declaration. A separate merit list is prepared for NISER and CEBS based on the candidate's score. The score is calculated using the marks from the General Section and the best three scores from the subject sections.
  3. Step 3: Counselling and Admission. Shortlisted candidates are called for counselling and admission to the 5-year Integrated M.Sc. program at either NISER or CEBS.

💰Stipend & Salary Potential

During the 5-year M.Sc. program: All admitted students are eligible to receive an annual scholarship of ₹60,000 and an annual contingency grant of ₹20,000 through the INSPIRE program of the Department of Science and Technology, Govt. of India.

After Graduation: Graduates typically pursue Ph.D.s or join R&D roles. Starting salary as a Scientist in a government lab (after Ph.D.) can be approximately ₹1,00,000 - ₹1,20,000 per month.

👨‍🔬Starting Post & Career Paths

The NEST exam leads to an academic program, not a direct job. The starting designation is Integrated M.Sc. Student. After completion, career paths include:

  • Pursuing a Ph.D. in India or abroad at top universities.
  • Working as a Scientist in government research organizations like BARC, DRDO, ISRO.
  • Joining R&D departments in private sector high-tech industries.
  • A career in science policy, communication, or management.

📋Job Profile (NA)

NA (This is an entrance exam for an integrated science program).

🎁Facilities After Selection (at NISER/CEBS)

  • Exposure to world-class faculty who are active researchers.
  • State-of-the-art research laboratories and advanced scientific equipment.
  • A curriculum that emphasizes early research exposure and interdisciplinary learning.
  • Fully residential campuses with excellent hostel and sports facilities.
  • Opportunities for internships at premier national and international research institutions.

🎯Scope

NEST is the sole gateway for admission to the prestigious 5-year Integrated M.Sc. program at the National Institute of Science Education and Research (NISER), Bhubaneswar and the University of Mumbai - Department of Atomic Energy Centre for Excellence in Basic Sciences (UM-DAE CEBS), Mumbai. It is one of the premier pathways for a student aspiring to become a research scientist in India.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The NEST exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically in June.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official NEST website. The process involves registration, filling the application form, uploading documents, and paying the application fee.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Section Type Marks
General MCQ 30
Physics MCQ & MSQ 50
Chemistry MCQ & MSQ 50
Mathematics MCQ & MSQ 50
Biology MCQ & MSQ 50
Total 230

Scoring Pattern: The exam has a complex scoring pattern with negative marking for incorrect MCQs and a different marking scheme for Multiple Select Questions (MSQs). The final merit score is calculated from the General section plus the **best three** subject sections.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 210 minutes (3 hours and 30 minutes).

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 2 to 3 weeks after the examination, usually in July.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically ₹1,400 for male candidates (General/OBC) and ₹700 for female candidates and SC/ST/PwD candidates. This fee is subject to change.
