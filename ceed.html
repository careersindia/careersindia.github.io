ℹ️Full Form

Common Entrance Examination for Design (CEED).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: A candidate must have completed a degree/diploma/postgraduate degree program of a minimum of 3 years (after 10+2 level).
  • Discipline: There is **no restriction** on the discipline of the undergraduate degree. Candidates from Engineering, Architecture, Fine Arts, and other fields are all eligible.
  • Final Year Students: Candidates appearing for their final degree examination are also eligible to apply.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** to appear for the CEED exam.

🎨Detailed Syllabus

The exam has two parts, testing a variety of skills:

  • Part A (Objective): Visualization & Spatial Ability, Environmental & Social Awareness, Analytical & Logical Reasoning, Language & Creativity, Design Thinking & Problem Solving.
  • Part B (Subjective): Drawing, Creativity, Communication Skills, and Analytical & Problem-Solving Skills. This part requires sketching and drawing.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Appear for CEED Exam. All candidates must appear for both Part A and Part B.
  2. Step 2: Shortlisting (Part A). Part A is used as a screening test. Only candidates who clear the Part A cut-off will have their Part B answer sheets evaluated.
  3. Step 3: Merit Rank (Part B). The final CEED rank is based **only** on the score obtained in Part B.
  4. Step 4: Apply to Institutes. Qualifying in CEED does not guarantee admission. Candidates must apply separately to each participating institute (like IITs, IISc) using their CEED scorecard. These institutes will then conduct their own interviews and/or portfolio reviews for final selection.

💰Salary Potential (After M.Des)

A Master of Design (M.Des) from a top IIT or IISc is a highly valued qualification, leading to lucrative careers.

  • Average Salary Package: Approximately ₹18 Lakhs to ₹25 Lakhs per annum.
  • Highest Salary Package: Can go well above ₹50 Lakhs to ₹1 Crore per annum for top talent in UI/UX and product design roles at major tech companies.

💼Starting Post & Career Paths

Graduates begin their careers in high-impact design roles, including:

  • UI/UX Designer at companies like Google, Microsoft, Adobe, and startups.
  • Product Designer responsible for the look and feel of physical or digital products.
  • Industrial Designer in automotive, electronics, and consumer goods industries.
  • Interaction Designer focusing on how users interact with technology.
  • Design Researcher studying user behavior to inform design decisions.

📋Job Profile of a Designer

A designer's job is to solve problems creatively. The profile involves understanding user needs through research, ideating and brainstorming solutions, creating sketches and prototypes, testing designs with users, and collaborating with engineers and product managers to bring a product to life.

🎁Facilities After Selection (in an IIT/IISc)

  • Access to world-class design studios, prototyping labs, and workshops.
  • Guidance from renowned faculty who are leaders in the design field.
  • A highly collaborative and creative peer environment.
  • Strong industry linkages for internships and placements with top global companies.
  • Opportunities to work on real-world, sponsored design projects.

🎯Scope

CEED is the gateway for postgraduate (M.Des) and Ph.D. programs in design at premier institutions like the IITs and IISc Bangalore. A CEED qualification opens up a world of opportunities in the rapidly growing design industry, from leading tech innovation at FAANG companies to pioneering product development and design entrepreneurship.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The CEED exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically in the third week of January.

📝How to Apply

The application process is conducted online through the official CEED website, which is organized by IIT Bombay. The process involves registration, filling the application form, uploading documents, and paying the fee.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Part Type Mode Max Marks Duration
Part A Objective (MCQ, MSQ, NAT) Computer-Based 100 1 Hour
Part B Subjective (Drawing/Sketching) Pen & Paper 100 2 Hours

Scoring Pattern: Part A has no negative marking. Part B is evaluated subjectively. The final CEED score is the score obtained in Part B, provided the candidate qualifies in Part A.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 3 hours, with 1 hour for Part A and 2 hours for Part B, conducted in a single session.

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared in the first week of March, approximately 5-6 weeks after the exam.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹3,600 for male candidates (General/EWS/OBC) and ₹1,800 for female candidates and SC/ST/PwD candidates.
