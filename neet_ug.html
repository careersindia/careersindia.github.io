DETAILS ABOUT NEET-UG EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (Undergraduate).

✅Eligibility

  • Nationality: Must be an Indian Citizen, Non-Resident Indian (NRI), Overseas Citizen of India (OCI), Person of Indian Origin (PIO), or Foreign National.
  • Academic Qualification: Must have passed or be appearing in the 10+2 or equivalent examination with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology, and English as core subjects.
  • Marks: Minimum 50% aggregate marks in PCB for General category, 40% for SC/ST/OBC, and 45% for PwD.

🎂Age Limit

The candidate must have completed **17 years** of age at the time of admission or will complete that age on or before 31st December of the year of admission. There is **no upper age limit**.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The syllabus is based on the Class XI and XII curriculum of Physics, Chemistry, and Biology as prescribed by the National Medical Commission (NMC).

  • Physics: Physical World and Measurement, Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Work, Energy and Power, Thermodynamics, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects, Optics, Modern Physics.
  • Chemistry: Structure of Atom, Chemical Bonding, States of Matter, Thermodynamics, Equilibrium, Electrochemistry, Organic Chemistry (Hydrocarbons, Alcohols, etc.), Inorganic Chemistry (s, p, d, f block elements).
  • Biology: Diversity in Living World, Cell Structure, Plant Physiology, Human Physiology, Reproduction, Genetics and Evolution, Biology and Human Welfare, Biotechnology, Ecology and Environment.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: NEET-UG Written Examination. A single-day, pen-and-paper (OMR-based) offline examination.
  2. Step 2: Declaration of Results & All India Rank (AIR). The NTA declares the results and assigns an All India Rank to qualified candidates.
  3. Step 3: Counselling. Qualified candidates participate in online counselling for seat allotment. This is conducted separately by the DGHS for the 15% All India Quota (AIQ) and by respective state authorities for the 85% State Quota seats.

💰Salary Potential after MBBS

The salary of a doctor varies greatly based on specialization, experience, and place of work (government vs. private).

  • Government Hospital (Junior Resident): Approximately ₹60,000 - ₹90,000 per month during internship/residency.
  • Private Practice/Corporate Hospital: Can range from ₹1 Lakh to several Lakhs per month after specialization (MD/MS) and experience.

⚕️Starting Post & Career Paths

After completing the 5.5-year MBBS course (including a 1-year internship), graduates start as a Medical Intern / Junior Resident Doctor. Following this, career paths diverge:

  • Pursue postgraduate specialization (MD/MS) via NEET-PG.
  • Work as a General Physician or Medical Officer in government or private hospitals.
  • Join the Army Medical Corps (AMC).
  • Prepare for Civil Services (UPSC).
  • Pursue a career in medical research or hospital administration (MHA).

📋Job Profile

The job profile of a doctor involves diagnosing and treating illnesses, performing medical procedures, advising patients on health and wellness, and maintaining patient records. It is a profession that demands deep knowledge, compassion, and a commitment to lifelong learning.

🎁Facilities After Selection (in a Medical College)

  • Access to state-of-the-art medical laboratories, anatomy dissection halls, and simulation labs.
  • Clinical training in associated teaching hospitals with high patient inflow.
  • Extensive medical libraries with access to international journals.
  • Hostel facilities and a vibrant campus life with various academic and cultural events.

🎯Scope

The scope of medicine is vast and ever-expanding. NEET-UG is the gateway to a respected, stable, and service-oriented career as a doctor. It allows entry into MBBS, BDS (Dental), BAMS (Ayurveda), BHMS (Homeopathy), and other medical courses, leading to diverse specializations and a lifelong opportunity to make a difference in society.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The NEET-UG exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically on the first Sunday of May.

📝How to Apply

The application process is managed by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Candidates must apply online through the official NTA NEET website. The process involves registration, filling the application form, uploading documents (photograph, signature, etc.), and paying the fee online.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Subject Section A (35 Qs) Section B (15 Qs, attempt 10) Total Marks
Physics 140 40 180

Botany 140 40 180
Zoology 140 40 180
Total 200 Questions (attempt 180) 720

Scoring Pattern: +4 marks for every correct answer, -1 mark for every incorrect answer.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 200 minutes (3 hours and 20 minutes).

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 40 to 45 days after the examination date.

💸Fees

The application fee varies by category. It is typically around ₹1,700 for General category, ₹1,600 for EWS/OBC-NCL, and ₹1,000 for SC/ST/PwD candidates.
