ℹ️Full Form

Staff Selection Commission - Combined Graduate Level Examination.

✅Eligibility

  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India (or other eligible categories as per Govt. norms).
  • Academic Qualification: A Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University or equivalent. Specific qualifications are required for certain posts like JSO and AAO.

🎂Age Limit

The age limit varies depending on the post. It is generally between **18 and 32 years**. Common age groups are:

  • 18-27 years
  • 20-30 years
  • Up to 30 years
  • Up to 32 years

Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/PwD/Ex-Servicemen) as per government rules.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • General Intelligence & Reasoning: Verbal and non-verbal reasoning, including analogy, classification, coding-decoding, syllogism, and problem-solving.
  • General Awareness: Static GK (History, Geography, Polity, Economics, Science) and Current Affairs of national and international importance.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Arithmetic, Algebra, Geometry, Trigonometry, Mensuration, and Data Interpretation.
  • English Comprehension: Reading comprehension, vocabulary (synonyms/antonyms), grammar, sentence structure, error spotting, cloze test.
  • Tier-II Subjects: Includes advanced modules in Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning, English, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge, and specific papers for JSO (Statistics) and AAO (Finance & Economics).

⚙️Selection Process

The selection process is conducted in two tiers:

  1. Tier-I: Computer Based Examination (CBE). An objective multiple-choice exam that is qualifying in nature.
  2. Tier-II: Computer Based Examination (CBE). This is the main stage for merit calculation. It consists of multiple papers and includes a Computer Knowledge Test (CKT) and a Data Entry Speed Test (DEST), which are qualifying.
  3. Step 3: Document Verification. Candidates are called for document verification based on their performance in Tier-II.

💰Starting Salary Potential

The salary varies based on the Pay Level of the post (from Level 4 to Level 8). For a Group 'B' post in a metropolitan city, the in-hand starting salary is approximately ₹65,000 to ₹85,000 per month.

🏢Starting Post & Department

SSC CGL recruits for prestigious Group 'B' and Group 'C' posts in various Ministries and Departments of the Government of India. Some of the most sought-after posts include:

  • Assistant Section Officer (ASO) in Central Secretariat Service (CSS).
  • Inspector in CBDT (Income Tax), CBIC (Excise/Preventive Officer/Examiner).
  • Assistant Enforcement Officer (AEO) in Directorate of Enforcement (ED).
  • Sub Inspector in CBI and NIA.
  • Divisional Accountant under C&AG.

📋Job Profile

The job profile is diverse and depends on the post. It can range from clerical and administrative work (ASO), tax assessment and raids (Income Tax Inspector), intelligence gathering and investigation (CBI/NIA), to auditing and accounting (Divisional Accountant). It is a desk-based or field job with significant responsibilities.

🎁Facilities After Selection

  • A secure and prestigious central government job with a good salary.
  • Pension benefits under the National Pension System (NPS).
  • Medical facilities via the Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS).
  • Paid leaves, LTC, and access to government accommodation.

🎯Scope

SSC CGL is one of the most popular graduate-level exams in India. It is the gateway to a stable and respected career in the central government with excellent opportunities for growth. Through promotions, one can reach high-level gazetted officer posts over the course of their career.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The SSC-CGL exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Months: The exam cycle is long. The Tier-I exam is typically held in the latter half of the year.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the new official website of the Staff Selection Commission, using their One-Time Registration (OTR) profile.

💸Fees

The application fee is ₹100 for General/OBC male candidates. **Women candidates and candidates belonging to SC, ST, PwD, and Ex-servicemen categories are exempted from fee payment.**
