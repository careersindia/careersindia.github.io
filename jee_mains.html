DETAILS ABOUT JEE MAIN EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Joint Entrance Examination (Main).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: Must have passed the 10+2 or equivalent examination. Candidates appearing in the 10+2 examination are also eligible.
  • Subjects in 10+2: Must have passed with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with one from Chemistry/Biotechnology/Biology/Technical Vocational subject.
  • Year of Appearance: Candidates can appear for JEE Main for three consecutive years starting from the year they pass their 12th grade examination.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for the candidates. However, they need to fulfill the year of appearance criteria mentioned above.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The syllabus is based on the topics covered in Class XI and XII of Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.

  • Physics: Kinematics, Laws of Motion, Thermodynamics, Electrostatics, Current Electricity, Magnetic Effects of Current and Magnetism, Electromagnetic Induction, Optics, Dual Nature of Matter and Radiation, Atoms and Nuclei.
  • Chemistry:
    • Physical: Mole Concept, Atomic Structure, States of Matter, Thermodynamics, Equilibrium, Electrochemistry, Chemical Kinetics.
    • Organic: Hydrocarbons, Organic Compounds Containing Halogens/Oxygen/Nitrogen, Biomolecules, Polymers.
    • Inorganic: Classification of Elements, s, p, d, f block elements, Coordination Compounds, Environmental Chemistry.
  • Mathematics: Sets, Relations and Functions, Complex Numbers, Matrices and Determinants, Permutations and Combinations, Binomial Theorem, Sequences and Series, Calculus, Coordinate Geometry, Three Dimensional Geometry, Vector Algebra, Statistics and Probability, Trigonometry.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: JEE Main Examination. Candidates appear for the computer-based test in one or more sessions. The best score is considered.
  2. Step 2: Declaration of Results & All India Rank (AIR). The NTA declares the results and assigns an All India Rank based on the percentile score.
  3. Step 3: JoSAA/CSAB Counselling. Candidates with a qualifying rank participate in the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) counselling for seat allotment to NITs, IIITs, and other GFTIs.
  4. Step 4: Qualification for JEE Advanced. The top ~2.5 Lakh candidates in JEE Main are eligible to appear for the JEE Advanced exam for admission to the Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs).

💰Placement & Salary Potential (After B.Tech)

Graduates from top NITs and IIITs secure excellent placements in leading tech and core engineering companies.

  • Average Salary Package: Approximately ₹15 Lakhs to ₹25 Lakhs per annum in top NITs.
  • Highest Salary Package: Often exceeds ₹1 Crore per annum for top students securing international offers from tech giants.

🚀Starting Post & Career Paths

Graduates start their careers in a variety of high-demand roles, including:

  • Software Development Engineer (SDE) at top tech companies.
  • Data Scientist / Analyst.
  • Core Engineering Roles in Mechanical, Civil, Electrical, etc.
  • Consultant in management and tech consulting firms.
  • Product Manager or Analyst roles.

🎁Facilities After Selection (in NITs/IIITs)

  • High-quality academic infrastructure with modern labs and classrooms.
  • Extensive libraries with access to a vast collection of books and digital journals.
  • A competitive and vibrant campus environment with numerous technical clubs, fests, and competitions.
  • Excellent hostel and sports facilities.
  • Strong industry connections for internships and placements.

🎯Scope

JEE Main is the gateway to some of the best engineering colleges in India, including the National Institutes of Technology (NITs), Indian Institutes of Information Technology (IIITs), and other Government Funded Technical Institutions (GFTIs). A good rank opens up opportunities for a world-class engineering education, leading to high-paying jobs, research opportunities, and a pathway to higher studies in prestigious universities globally.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The JEE Main exam is conducted twice a year.
  • Exam Months: Typically in January (Session 1) and April (Session 2).

📝How to Apply

The application process is managed by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Candidates must apply online through the official NTA JEE Main website. The process involves registration, filling the application form, uploading documents, and paying the application fee online.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern (Paper 1: B.E./B.Tech)

Subject Section A (MCQs) Section B (Numerical) Total Marks
Physics 20 Questions 10 Questions (attempt 5) 100
Chemistry 20 Questions 10 Questions (attempt 5) 100
Mathematics 20 Questions 10 Questions (attempt 5) 100
Total 90 Questions (attempt 75) 300

Scoring Pattern: +4 marks for every correct answer, -1 mark for every incorrect answer (applicable to both sections).

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 180 minutes (3 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results for each session are declared very quickly, often within 10 to 15 days after the conclusion of the exam session.

💸Fees

The application fee varies by category, gender, and the number of papers. For Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech) in India, it is typically around ₹1,000 for male candidates (General/EWS/OBC) and ₹800 for female candidates and reserved categories.
