ℹ️Full Form

Indian Statistical Institute (ISI) Admission Test.

✅Eligibility (for UG Courses)

  • For B.Stat (Hons.): 10+2 or equivalent with Mathematics and English as subjects.
  • For B.Math (Hons.): 10+2 or equivalent with Mathematics and English as subjects.
  • Candidates appearing in their final 10+2 examination are also eligible.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for appearing in the ISI Admission Test.

🔬Detailed Syllabus

The exam tests mathematical ingenuity and problem-solving skills, far beyond standard board-level questions. The syllabus is based on high school mathematics.

  • Mathematics: Algebra (complex numbers, theory of equations, matrices), Coordinate Geometry, Trigonometry, Calculus, and a strong emphasis on Number Theory, Geometry, and Combinatorics.
  • The questions are similar in style to Mathematics Olympiads, requiring deep conceptual understanding and creativity.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Written Test. This is a single-day, two-part offline test.
    • Part 1: Objective Test (MCQs).
    • Part 2: Subjective Test (Descriptive Problems).
  2. Step 2: Interview. Candidates are shortlisted based on their performance in both parts of the written test and are called for a personal interview.
  3. Step 3: Final Merit List. The final selection is based on the combined performance in the written test and the interview.

💰Stipend & Salary Potential

During the UG program: All admitted students receive a **monthly stipend of ₹5,000** and a contingency grant.

After Graduation: ISI graduates are among the highest-paid in the country, especially in niche quantitative roles.

  • Average Salary Package: Approximately ₹25 Lakhs to ₹35 Lakhs per annum.
  • Highest Salary Package: Regularly exceeds ₹1 Crore per annum for roles in high-frequency trading and quantitative finance.

🚀Starting Post & Career Paths

ISI is a launchpad for elite careers in academia and industry:

  • Pursuing a Ph.D. in Mathematics or Statistics at the world's top universities (MIT, Princeton, Harvard).
  • Quantitative Analyst ("Quant") at global hedge funds and investment banks like Jane Street, Tower Research, Goldman Sachs.
  • Data Scientist / Research Scientist at top tech firms like Google, Microsoft.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The ISI Admission Test is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically held in May.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official ISI admissions portal. The process involves filling the application form, choosing test centers, and paying the application fee.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern (for UG)

Part Type Duration
Part 1 (Morning Session) Objective (MCQ) 2 Hours
Part 2 (Afternoon Session) Subjective (Descriptive Math Problems) 2 Hours

Scoring Pattern: The objective part has **negative marking** for incorrect answers. The subjective part requires detailed, proof-based solutions, and marks are awarded for correct steps and reasoning. Both parts are crucial for shortlisting.

⏱️Duration of the Exam (NA)

NA (The exam is held in two separate 2-hour sessions on the same day).

⏳Result Time

The results of the written test are typically declared in June, followed by interview calls. Final results are announced in July.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹1,500 for male candidates (General) and ₹750-1000 for female candidates and reserved categories. This fee is subject to change.
