DETAILS ABOUT JEE ADVANCED EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Joint Entrance Examination (Advanced).

✅Eligibility

  • Performance in JEE (Main): Candidates must be among the top ~2,50,000 successful candidates (including all categories) in the B.E./B.Tech. paper of JEE (Main).
  • Age Limit: Candidates should have been born on or after October 1, 1999. Five years relaxation is given to SC, ST, and PwD candidates.
  • Number of Attempts: A candidate can attempt JEE (Advanced) a maximum of two times in two consecutive years.
  • Appearance in Class XII (or equivalent): A candidate should have appeared for the Class XII (or equivalent) examination for the first time in either the preceding year or the current year.
  • Earlier Admission at IITs: A candidate should NOT have been admitted to an IIT before.

🎂Age Limit

Yes, there is an age limit. Candidates must be born on or after October 1, 1999 (age relaxation applies for reserved categories).

📖Detailed Syllabus

The syllabus is based on the Class XI and XII curriculum of Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, but the scope is deeper and more comprehensive than JEE Main.

  • Physics: Includes topics like General Physics, Mechanics, Thermal Physics, Electricity & Magnetism, Optics, and Modern Physics, with a strong emphasis on conceptual understanding and complex problem-solving.
  • Chemistry: Covers Physical, Organic, and Inorganic Chemistry. The syllabus is extensive, requiring a deep understanding of reaction mechanisms, thermodynamics, and the properties of elements and compounds.
  • Mathematics: Includes Algebra, Trigonometry, Analytical Geometry, Differential Calculus, Integral Calculus, and Vectors. Questions are known for being non-standard and requiring a high degree of analytical skill.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Qualify in JEE (Main). Be among the top ~2.5 Lakh candidates.
  2. Step 2: Register and Appear for JEE (Advanced). This consists of two computer-based papers (Paper 1 and Paper 2) held on the same day.
  3. Step 3: Declaration of Results & All India Rank (AIR). Ranks are prepared based on the aggregate marks obtained in Paper 1 and Paper 2.
  4. Step 4: JoSAA Counselling. Candidates who secure a rank participate in the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) counselling for admission to the Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs).

💰Placement & Salary Potential (After B.Tech from IIT)

Placements at top IITs (Bombay, Delhi, Madras, etc.) are among the best in the world, attracting top global companies.

  • Average Salary Package: Approximately ₹25 Lakhs to ₹35 Lakhs per annum.
  • Highest Salary Package: Frequently exceeds ₹1.5 to ₹2 Crores per annum, especially for international roles in quantitative finance and software engineering.

🚀Starting Post & Career Paths

IIT graduates are recruited for premier roles across industries:

  • Software Development Engineer at FAANG companies (Facebook/Meta, Amazon, Apple, Netflix, Google).
  • Quantitative Analyst ("Quant") at high-frequency trading firms and investment banks.
  • Management Consultant at top-tier firms like McKinsey, BCG, and Bain.
  • Core Engineering Roles in R&D departments of leading global firms.
  • Many also become successful Entrepreneurs, founding major startups.

🎯Scope

JEE Advanced is the sole gateway to the 23 Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs), which are institutions of national importance and globally recognized centers of excellence. A degree from an IIT provides unparalleled opportunities for careers in cutting-edge technology, research, finance, and entrepreneurship. It is considered the pinnacle of undergraduate engineering education in India.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The JEE Advanced exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically held in late May or early June, after the JEE Main results are finalized.

📝How to Apply

Only candidates who qualify in JEE (Main) are eligible to apply. The registration process is done online through the official JEE Advanced website, which is managed by one of the IITs on a rotational basis each year.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

The exam structure is **not fixed** and changes every year, which is a key part of its challenge. It always consists of two papers, but the type of questions can vary.

Parameter Details
Number of Papers 2 (Paper 1 and Paper 2, both compulsory)
Subjects Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics in both papers
Question Types Can include MCQs with single correct, MCQs with multiple correct, Numerical Answer Type, Paragraph-based questions, Matrix Match, etc.
Scoring Complex partial and negative marking schemes are a hallmark of the exam.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

Each paper (Paper 1 and Paper 2) is 3 hours (180 minutes) long. Both are held on the same day with a break in between.

⏳Result Time

The results are declared very quickly, typically within 10 to 12 days after the examination date.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹2,900 for male candidates (General) and ₹1,450 for female candidates and reserved categories.
