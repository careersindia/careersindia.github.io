DETAILS ABOUT SSB INTERVIEW

ℹ️Full Form

Services Selection Board (SSB).

✅Eligibility

Candidates are eligible for an SSB interview call after successfully qualifying in a written examination like **NDA, CDS, AFCAT**, or through direct entry schemes like **TES (Technical Entry Scheme)** and **TGC (Technical Graduate Course)**.

🎂Age Limit

The age limit is determined by the specific entry scheme for which the candidate has applied (e.g., 16.5-19.5 years for NDA, 19-25 years for CDS-OTA, etc.).

🧠Syllabus (Qualities & Tests Assessed)

There is no academic syllabus. SSB is a personality and aptitude assessment that evaluates "Officer Like Qualities" (OLQs). The tests are designed to assess the candidate's mind, body, and actions through three major techniques:

  • Manasa (Psychological Tests): To assess the subconscious mind. Includes Thematic Apperception Test (TAT), Word Association Test (WAT), Situation Reaction Test (SRT), and Self-Description Test (SDT).
  • Vacha (Group Tests): To assess group dynamics and individual performance. Includes Group Discussion (GD), Group Planning Exercise (GPE), Progressive Group Task (PGT), and Individual Obstacles.
  • Karma (Interview): A personal interview with a senior officer to assess personality, general awareness, and suitability for a career in the armed forces.

⚙️Selection Process (The 5-Day Procedure)

The SSB interview is a comprehensive 5-day process:

  • Day 0 (Reporting): Document verification and form filling.
  • Day 1 (Screening Test): Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR) test and Picture Perception & Discussion Test (PP&DT). Candidates who clear this are "Screened In" and stay for the next four days.
  • Day 2 (Psychological Tests): A battery of written psychological tests (TAT, WAT, SRT, SDT).
  • Day 3 & 4 (Group Testing Officer - GTO Tasks): A series of indoor and outdoor group tasks, including GD, GPE, PGT, Command Task, and Individual Obstacles. The Personal Interview is also conducted on one of these days.
  • Day 5 (Conference): All assessors (the Psychologist, GTO, and Interviewing Officer) discuss each candidate's performance. The final result ("Recommended" or "Not Recommended") is declared.

💰Starting Salary Potential (After Clearing SSB & Training)

NA. SSB is a selection process. The salary is awarded after completing training at the respective military academy. As a commissioned officer (Lieutenant, Sub-Lieutenant, or Flying Officer), the starting salary is approximately ₹85,000 - ₹1,00,000 per month.

🎖️Starting Post & Department (After Clearing SSB & Training)

NA. The starting post is determined by the entry scheme and training academy. It will be a commissioned officer rank in the Indian Army, Navy, or Air Force.

📋Job Profile (NA)

NA. This is a selection process for officer roles in the Indian Armed Forces.

🎁Facilities During SSB

  • Free accommodation and messing (food) is provided to all candidates for the duration of the 5-day interview.
  • Candidates appearing for the first time are entitled to a travel allowance (AC-III Tier train fare by the shortest route).

🎯Scope

The SSB interview is the universal and mandatory gateway to becoming a commissioned officer in the Indian Armed Forces. Clearing the SSB is a prestigious achievement that opens the door to a challenging, adventurous, and honorable career dedicated to leading the men and women of the Army, Navy, or Air Force.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: SSB interviews are conducted throughout the year.
  • Dates: After clearing a written exam (like NDA/CDS), candidates receive a "call-up letter" and can select a date from a batch of available dates at their allotted SSB center.

📝How to Apply

Candidates do not apply for the SSB directly. They apply for an officer entry scheme (e.g., NDA, CDS, TGC). If they clear the initial shortlisting or written exam, they are automatically called for the SSB interview.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

There is no numerical score given to the candidate. The assessment is holistic and qualitative.

Stage Outcome
Day 1 (Screening) Screened In / Screened Out
Day 5 (Conference) Recommended / Not Recommended

Only "Recommended" candidates proceed to the medical examination.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The entire SSB process lasts for 5 days.

⏳Result Time

The result of the SSB interview is declared on the **final day (Day 5)**, immediately after the conference.

💸Fees

There is **no fee** for appearing for the SSB interview. Travel allowance is provided to first-time candidates.

🌐Official Website

There is no single SSB website. Candidates should refer to the official career websites of the respective forces:

  • Indian Army: joinindianarmy.nic.in
  • Indian Navy: joinindiannavy.gov.in
  • Indian Air Force: careerindianairforce.cdac.in
