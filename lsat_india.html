DETAILS ABOUT LSAT EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Law School Admission Test (LSAT—India).

✅Eligibility

  • For 5-Year B.A. LL.B.: Must have passed or be appearing in the 10+2 or equivalent examination.
  • For 3-Year LL.B.: Must have a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline.
  • There is no minimum percentage requirement to appear for the LSAT, but participating colleges have their own criteria.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for candidates to appear for the LSAT—India exam.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The LSAT is a test of high-level reasoning and comprehension skills, not of prior knowledge. There is **no Mathematics or General Knowledge section**.

  • Analytical Reasoning: Questions based on "logic games" that require you to deduce conclusions from a given set of rules or relationships.
  • Logical Reasoning (Two Sections): Analyzing, evaluating, and completing arguments presented in short passages.
  • Reading Comprehension: Reading and understanding long, dense passages on complex topics, and answering questions based on them.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Appear for the LSAT—India Exam. This is a remote proctored online test.
  2. Step 2: Receive Scorecard. Candidates receive a score on a scale of 420-480 and a percentile rank.
  3. Step 3: Apply to Law Schools. Candidates must use their LSAT—India scorecard to apply **separately** to each participating law school (like JGLS, UPES, Bennett University, etc.).
  4. Step 4: College-specific Selection. Each law school conducts its own final selection process, which may include a personal interview, written test, or portfolio review.

💰Salary Potential (After graduation from a top LSAT college)

Graduates from top private law schools like Jindal Global Law School (JGLS) are highly recruited by top-tier corporate law firms.

  • Average Salary Package: Approximately ₹15 Lakhs to ₹20 Lakhs per annum.
  • Highest Salary Package: Can exceed ₹30 Lakhs per annum.

⚖️Starting Post & Career Paths (NA)

NA (This is an entrance exam for an undergraduate/postgraduate course). Graduates pursue the same career paths as those from top NLUs, such as Associate in a law firm, in-house counsel, etc.

📋Job Profile (NA)

NA (This is an entrance exam for a law degree).

🎁Facilities After Selection (in LSAT Colleges)

  • World-class infrastructure with a global outlook.
  • Faculty often includes professors with degrees from international universities.
  • A strong focus on international law, with many opportunities for global exchange programs.
  • Excellent placement cells with strong connections to both Indian and international law firms.

🎯Scope

The LSAT—India is the premier entrance exam for admission to a consortium of top private law schools in India, most notably Jindal Global Law School (JGLS). It is ideal for students who excel in critical reasoning and reading comprehension over rote memorization of GK or Math formulas. A high score is a gateway to a global-standard legal education and an elite corporate law career.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The LSAT—India is conducted **multiple times a year**.
  • Exam Months: Typically, there are two main sessions in January and May/June.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must register and apply online through the official LSAT—India website managed by the Law School Admission Council (LSAC).

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Section Approx. No. of Questions Time Allotted
Analytical Reasoning 23 35 Minutes
Logical Reasoning 1 22 35 Minutes
Logical Reasoning 2 23 35 Minutes
Reading Comprehension 24 35 Minutes
Total ~92 140 Minutes

Scoring Pattern: The raw score is converted to a scaled score between 420 and 480 and a percentile rank. There is **no negative marking** for incorrect answers.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 2 hours and 20 minutes (140 minutes), with strict sectional time limits.

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 2 to 3 weeks after the examination date.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹3,999. This fee is subject to change. Application fees for individual law schools are separate.
