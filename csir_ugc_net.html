DETAILS ABOUT CSIR UGC NET EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Council of Scientific & Industrial Research - University Grants Commission National Eligibility Test (CSIR UGC NET).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: M.Sc. or equivalent degree / Integrated BS-MS / BS-4 years / B.E. / B.Tech / B.Pharma / MBBS with at least 55% marks for General/OBC and 50% for SC/ST/PwD candidates.
  • Candidates enrolled for M.Sc. or having completed 10+2+3 years of the above qualifying examination are also eligible to apply.

🎂Age Limit

  • For JRF (Junior Research Fellowship): Maximum **28 years**. Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories (SC/ST/PwD/Female) as per rules.
  • For LS (Lectureship/Assistant Professor): There is **no upper age limit**.

🔬Detailed Syllabus

The exam is conducted for five science subjects. The syllabus for each is based on the M.Sc. curriculum.

  • Part A (Common to all): General Aptitude with emphasis on Logical Reasoning, Graphical Analysis, Analytical and Numerical Ability, and Quantitative Comparison.
  • Part B & C (Subject Specific): Questions from the chosen subject:
    1. Chemical Sciences
    2. Earth, Atmospheric, Ocean and Planetary Sciences
    3. Life Sciences
    4. Mathematical Sciences
    5. Physical Sciences

⚙️Selection Process

CSIR UGC NET is an eligibility test. The process is straightforward:

  1. Step 1: Appear for the Computer-Based Test.
  2. Step 2: Qualify the Exam. Two separate merit lists are prepared: one for candidates qualifying for the award of JRF and the second for candidates qualifying for Lectureship/Assistant Professor (LS).
  3. Step 3: Use the Qualification.
    • JRF Awardees: Can enroll in a Ph.D. program with a fellowship at any recognized Indian university or research institute.
    • LS Qualifiers: Fulfill the eligibility criteria to apply for the post of Assistant Professor in colleges and universities.

💰Stipend / Salary Potential

For JRF Awardees (During Ph.D.):

  • Junior Research Fellow (JRF): A monthly stipend of approximately ₹37,000 for the first two years.
  • Senior Research Fellow (SRF): A monthly stipend of approximately ₹42,000 for the remaining tenure, plus HRA.

After Ph.D. / For LS (As Assistant Professor): The starting salary in a central university is approximately ₹80,000 - ₹95,000 per month.

🧑‍🔬Starting Post

This is an eligibility test. After qualifying, the starting designation is:

  • Junior Research Fellow (JRF) / Ph.D. Scholar in a research institution.
  • Assistant Professor in a college or university (after a separate recruitment process).

📋Job Profile (of a JRF/Ph.D. Scholar)

The profile involves conducting original scientific research, publishing findings in academic journals, attending and presenting at conferences, and often assisting in teaching undergraduate and postgraduate students as a Teaching Assistant (TA).

🎁Facilities After Selection (in a Research Institute)

NA (This is an eligibility test. Facilities depend on the institute where the candidate joins for their Ph.D.).

🎯Scope

The CSIR UGC NET is the most prestigious eligibility test for a career in scientific research and academia in India. Qualifying for JRF is the primary pathway to a fully-funded Ph.D. in India's top scientific institutions. Qualifying for LS is the mandatory eligibility criterion to become an Assistant Professor in the science faculties of Indian colleges and universities.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The exam is conducted twice a year.
  • Exam Months: Typically in June and December.

📝How to Apply

The application process is managed by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Candidates must apply online through the official NTA CSIR UGC NET website.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Part Subject Nature of Questions
Part A General Aptitude 20 Qs (Attempt 15)
Part B Subject Specific (MCQ) 35-50 Qs (Attempt varies)
Part C Subject Specific (Higher-order skills) 20-30 Qs (Attempt varies)

Total Marks: 200.

Scoring Pattern: There is **negative marking** of 25% for each incorrect answer in all three parts.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the single-paper exam is 180 minutes (3 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 1 month after the examination.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹1,150 for the General category, with lower fees for EWS/OBC and SC/ST/PwD candidates. This fee is subject to change.

🌐Official Website

For all official notifications, application forms, and authentic information, always refer to the official NTA CSIR NET website: csirnet.nta.nic.in
