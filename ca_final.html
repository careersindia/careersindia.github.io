ℹ️Full Form

Chartered Accountant - Final Course. This is the third and final level of the Chartered Accountancy course offered by ICAI.

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: Must have cleared both groups of the CA Intermediate Examination.
  • Practical Training: Must have completed or be in the last six months of their mandatory 3-year Articleship (Practical Training).

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for appearing in the CA Final exam.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The course is divided into two groups with three papers each, testing expert-level knowledge:

  • Group I:
    1. Financial Reporting
    2. Advanced Financial Management
    3. Advanced Auditing, Assurance and Professional Ethics
  • Group II:
    1. Direct Tax Laws & International Taxation
    2. Indirect Tax Laws
    3. Integrated Business Solutions (a multi-disciplinary case study paper)

⚙️Path to Becoming a CA

Passing CA Final is the last and toughest academic hurdle:

  1. Pass CA Foundation.
  2. Clear both groups of CA Intermediate.
  3. Complete 3-year Articleship.
  4. Pass both groups of CA Final.
  5. Become a Member of ICAI to be designated as a 'Chartered Accountant'.

💰Starting Salary Potential (After Qualifying)

A newly qualified Chartered Accountant is a highly valued professional in the finance industry.

  • Average Campus Placement Salary: Approximately ₹9 Lakhs to ₹15 Lakhs per annum.
  • Highest Salary (Rank Holders/International): Can range from ₹30 Lakhs to over ₹1 Crore per annum.

🏢Starting Post & Department

Qualified CAs begin their careers in various high-responsibility roles:

  • Audit & Assurance Associate in Big 4 firms (Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG).
  • Tax Consultant (Direct or Indirect Taxation).
  • Financial Analyst or Finance Manager in a company's finance department.
  • Credit Manager in banks.
  • They can also start their **own independent practice**.

📋Job Profile

A Chartered Accountant is a high-level professional responsible for a company's financial health and compliance. The role involves financial reporting, auditing, taxation, corporate finance, strategic financial planning, and advising the management and board of directors.

🎁Benefits of the CA Qualification

  • One of the most prestigious professional qualifications in the field of finance and commerce.
  • High demand across all industries, ensuring excellent career stability and growth.
  • Deep expertise in finance, accounting, taxation, and corporate law.
  • Global career opportunities, as the qualification is recognized in many countries.
  • Strong potential for entrepreneurship by starting an independent practice.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The CA Final exam is conducted **three times a year**.
  • Exam Months: Typically in May/June, September, and January.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must first register for the Final course with ICAI through their Self-Service Portal (SSP) after clearing Intermediate. Subsequently, they must fill a separate examination form for each attempt.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Paper Type Max Marks
All 6 Papers 30% Objective (MCQ) & 70% Subjective (case-study based) 100 per paper

Scoring Pattern: To pass a group, a candidate must obtain a minimum of **40% marks in each paper** AND a minimum of **50% marks in the aggregate** of that group. There is **negative marking of 0.25 marks** for each wrong answer in the objective (MCQ) part of all papers.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The duration for each of the six papers is 3 hours (180 minutes).

⏳Result Time

Results for the May/June exams are typically declared in July, and results for the November/January exams are declared in January/February, respectively. This is usually within **1.5 to 2 months**.

💸Fees

The fee structure involves a one-time course registration fee (around ₹22,000) and a separate examination fee for each attempt (around ₹1,800 for one group or ₹3,300 for both). These fees are subject to change by ICAI.
