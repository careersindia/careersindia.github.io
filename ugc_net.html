DETAILS ABOUT UGC-NET EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

University Grants Commission - National Eligibility Test (UGC-NET).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: Master's Degree or equivalent from a recognized university.
  • Marks: A minimum of 55% aggregate marks for General/EWS category and 50% for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/PwD).
  • Final year students of a Master's degree are also eligible to apply.

🎂Age Limit

  • For JRF (Junior Research Fellowship): Maximum **30 years**. Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories and women.
  • For Assistant Professor: There is **no upper age limit**.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The exam consists of two papers:

  • Paper 1 (Common for all): Tests Teaching Aptitude, Research Aptitude, Reading Comprehension, Communication, Reasoning, Data Interpretation, ICT, People & Environment, and Higher Education System.
  • Paper 2 (Subject Specific): Based on the Master's level syllabus of the subject chosen by the candidate from a list of 83 subjects (e.g., Economics, History, Political Science, Commerce, English Literature, etc.).

⚙️Selection Process

UGC-NET is an eligibility test. The process is straightforward:

  1. Step 1: Appear for the Computer-Based Test (CBT). Both Paper 1 and Paper 2 are conducted in a single session.
  2. Step 2: Qualify the Exam. Two separate merit lists are prepared:
    • List 1: For "Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) & Assistant Professor". This has a higher cut-off.
    • List 2: For "Assistant Professor only".
  3. Step 3: Use the Qualification. A qualified candidate can use their eligibility certificate to apply for Ph.D. programs or faculty positions.

💰Stipend / Salary Potential

For JRF Awardees (During Ph.D.):

  • Junior Research Fellow (JRF): A monthly stipend of approximately ₹37,000 for the first two years.
  • Senior Research Fellow (SRF): A monthly stipend of approximately ₹42,000 for the remaining tenure, plus HRA.

For Assistant Professor: The starting salary in a central university is approximately ₹70,000 - ₹90,000 per month (as per 7th Pay Commission, Level 10).

🧑‍🏫Starting Post

This is an eligibility test. After qualifying, the starting designation is:

  • Junior Research Fellow (JRF) / Ph.D. Scholar in a university or research institution.
  • Assistant Professor in a college or university (after a separate recruitment process).

📋Job Profile (of an Assistant Professor)

The profile involves teaching undergraduate and postgraduate students, conducting research and publishing academic papers, guiding student projects, and contributing to the administrative activities of the department and university.

🎁Facilities After Selection (NA)

NA (This is an eligibility test. Facilities depend on the university or college the candidate joins).

🎯Scope

The UGC-NET is the mandatory qualifying exam for anyone aspiring to become an Assistant Professor in Indian universities and colleges. It is also the primary gateway to a fully-funded Ph.D. in Arts, Humanities, and Social Sciences through the Junior Research Fellowship (JRF). It is the essential credential for a career in academia in these fields.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The exam is conducted twice a year.
  • Exam Months: Typically in June and December.

📝How to Apply

The application process is managed by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Candidates must apply online through the official NTA UGC-NET website.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Paper Subject No. of Questions Maximum Marks
Paper I Teaching & Research Aptitude 50 100
Paper II Subject Specific 100 200
Total 150 300

Scoring Pattern: +2 marks for every correct answer. There is **no negative marking** for incorrect answers.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 3 hours (180 minutes), conducted in a single session with no break between Paper I and Paper II.

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 1 month after the examination.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹1,150 for the General category, with lower fees for EWS/OBC and SC/ST/PwD candidates. This fee is subject to change.

🌐Official Website

For all official notifications, application forms, and authentic information, always refer to the official NTA UGC-NET website: ugcnet.nta.nic.in
