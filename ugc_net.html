UGC NET – University Grants Commission National Eligibility Test

Eligibility
Candidates must have a Master's degree or equivalent with at least 55% marks (50% for reserved categories) from a recognized university.
Scope
To determine the eligibility of Indian nationals for 'Assistant Professor' and for 'Junior Research Fellowship (JRF) and Assistant Professor' in Indian universities and colleges.
How to Apply
Online application via the official National Testing Agency (NTA) UGC NET website.
Exam Structure
Computer-Based Test (CBT) consisting of two papers. Paper 1 (General Paper on Teaching/Research Aptitude) has 50 questions, and Paper 2 (Subject-specific) has 100 questions. Total duration: 3 hours, without any break between papers.
Score Pattern
Each question carries 2 marks. There is no negative marking for incorrect answers.
Fees
Approximately ₹1,150 for General category; fees are subsidized for EWS/OBC/SC/ST/PwD categories.
Exam Timing
Conducted twice a year, typically in June and December cycles.
Results
Announced on the official NTA website, usually within a month of the exam conclusion.
