ℹ️Full Form

Employees' Provident Fund Organisation - Enforcement Officer (EO) / Accounts Officer (AO). This is a combined recruitment test conducted by UPSC.

✅Eligibility

  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India.
  • Academic Qualification: Bachelor's Degree in **any subject** from a recognized University. A degree in Law, an integrated 5-year Law degree, MBA, or CA/CS is a desirable (but not essential) qualification.

🎂Age Limit

The candidate's age must not exceed **30 years**. Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/Government Servants) as per UPSC rules.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • General English: To test the candidate's understanding of the English language and workmanlike use of words.
  • Indian Freedom Struggle.
  • Current Events and Developmental Issues.
  • Indian Polity & Economy.
  • General Accounting Principles.
  • Industrial Relations & Labour Laws. (This is a unique and important section).
  • General Science & knowledge of Computer Applications.
  • General Mental Ability & Quantitative Aptitude.
  • Social Security in India.

⚙️Selection Process

The selection is a two-stage process:

  1. Step 1: Recruitment Test (RT). An offline, pen-and-paper objective test. Marks from this test are used for shortlisting for the interview.
  2. Step 2: Interview. Candidates shortlisted from the RT are called for a personal interview.

The final merit list is prepared based on a **75:25 weightage** for marks from the Recruitment Test and the Interview, respectively.

💰Starting Salary Potential

This is a Level-8, Group 'B' Non-Ministerial Gazetted post. The in-hand starting salary, including all allowances in a metropolitan city, is approximately ₹85,000 - ₹95,000 per month.

🏢Starting Post & Department

The starting post is **Enforcement Officer (EO)** or **Accounts Officer (AO)** in the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), under the Ministry of Labour and Employment, Govt. of India.

📋Job Profile

  • Enforcement Officer: Involves field work, conducting inspections of establishments, ensuring compliance with the EPF Act, processing claims, and handling legal cases.
  • Accounts Officer: A desk-based role involving the maintenance of cash books, ledgers, and financial records of the organization.

🎁Facilities After Selection

  • A prestigious Gazetted Officer post in the central government.
  • Pension benefits under the National Pension System (NPS).
  • Medical facilities via the Central Government Health Scheme (CGHS).
  • Paid leaves, Leave Travel Concession (LTC), and access to government accommodation.

🎯Scope

A career as an EPFO EO/AO is highly respected and offers excellent work-life balance compared to many other government jobs. It provides a clear and fast promotional track. The next promotion is to the post of **Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner (APFC)**, which is a Group 'A' Gazetted post with a significant jump in salary and responsibilities.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: This exam is **not conducted annually**. It is held by UPSC based on the vacancies reported by EPFO, often with a gap of several years between notifications.
  • Exam Months: The exam schedule varies greatly depending on the recruitment cycle.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official UPSC website's Online Recruitment Application (ORA) portal when the notification is released.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Parameter Details
Exam Mode Offline, Pen & Paper (OMR-based)
Number of Questions 120 (Multiple Choice Questions)
Maximum Marks 300

Scoring Pattern: There is **negative marking** of 1/3rd of the marks for each incorrect answer.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the written test is 120 minutes (2 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results of the written test are typically declared within 1 to 2 months. The final result after the interview is declared thereafter.

💸Fees

The application fee is very nominal, typically **₹25**. **Female candidates and candidates belonging to SC, ST, and PwD categories are exempted from fee payment.**

🌐Official Website
