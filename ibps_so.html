DETAILS ABOUT IBPS SO EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Institute of Banking Personnel Selection - Specialist Officer (IBPS SO).

✅Eligibility

Eligibility is specific to the post. Common requirements include:

  • IT Officer: 4-year Engineering/Technology Degree in Computer Science/IT or a postgraduate degree in a relevant field.
  • Agricultural Field Officer: 4-year Degree (graduation) in Agriculture or allied specializations.
  • Law Officer: Bachelor Degree in Law (LLB) and enrolled as an advocate with the Bar Council.
  • Marketing Officer: MBA (Marketing) or equivalent PGDM.
  • HR/Personnel Officer: Graduate and a full-time Postgraduate degree or diploma in Personnel Management / HR.

🎂Age Limit

The candidate must be between **20 and 30 years** of age. Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/PwD) as per government rules.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • Prelims (Common): Reasoning, English Language, and either Quantitative Aptitude or General Awareness depending on the post.
  • Mains (Professional Knowledge): This is the most critical part. The syllabus is based on the professional subject of the specific post. For example:
    • IT Officer: Database Management, Data Communication & Networking, Operating Systems, Software Engineering, and Computer Organization.
    • Law Officer: Banking Regulation Act, RBI Act, SARFAESI Act, and other corporate & banking laws.
    • Marketing Officer: Marketing concepts, Branding, Advertising, Sales Management, and Market Research.

⚙️Selection Process

The selection process is conducted in three phases:

  1. Phase-I: Preliminary Examination. An online objective test that is qualifying in nature.
  2. Phase-II: Main Examination. An online objective test focusing on Professional Knowledge. Marks from this phase are considered for shortlisting for the interview.
  3. Phase-III: Interview. Candidates shortlisted from the Main Examination are called for a personal interview conducted by the participating banks.

💰Starting Salary Potential

An IBPS Specialist Officer is a Scale-I Officer post. The starting in-hand salary, including all allowances in a metropolitan city, is approximately ₹65,000 - ₹75,000 per month.

🏦Starting Post & Department

The starting post is **Specialist Officer (Scale-I)** in one of the participating Public Sector Banks. The department is specific to the role, such as:

  • Information Technology (IT) Department
  • Legal Department
  • Marketing Department
  • Human Resources (HR) Department
  • Agricultural Lending Department

📋Job Profile

The job profile is highly specialized. An **IT Officer** manages the bank's IT infrastructure. A **Law Officer** handles legal matters and documentation. A **Marketing Officer** is responsible for promoting the bank's products. An **Agricultural Field Officer** appraises and manages rural and agricultural loans.

🎁Facilities After Selection

  • A prestigious officer-level job in the public sector banking industry.
  • Pension benefits under the National Pension System (NPS).
  • Medical benefits, Leave Travel Concession (LTC), and other allowances.
  • Access to concessional rates on loans.
  • Leased accommodation/House Rent Allowance.

🎯Scope

IBPS SO is a fantastic opportunity for candidates with professional qualifications to enter the banking sector directly at the officer level. It offers a stable and rewarding career with a clear promotional path, allowing a Scale-I Officer to rise to senior management positions (Chief Manager, AGM, DGM, etc.) over time.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The IBPS SO exam is conducted annually.
  • Exam Months: The Preliminary exam is typically held in December, the Main exam in January, and interviews in February/March.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official IBPS website. The process involves registration, filling the detailed application form, uploading documents, and paying the application fee online.
