ℹ️Full Form

All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE).

🏫Classes Offered

Admission is open for two classes:

  • Class VI
  • Class IX

✅Eligibility

  • For Class VI: Both boys and girls can apply.
  • For Class IX: Only boys can apply. Candidate must have passed Class VIII from a recognized school.

🎂Age Limit

  • For Class VI: A candidate should be between 10 and 12 years of age as on 31st March of the year of admission.
  • For Class IX: A candidate should be between 13 and 15 years of age as on 31st March of the year of admission.

📖Detailed Syllabus

For Class VI:

  • Mathematics: Topics from Class V CBSE syllabus, including Number System, LCM & HCF, Fractions, Decimals, Average, Percentage, Profit & Loss, Simple Interest, Geometry, and Mensuration.
  • Intelligence: Analogies, Patterns (spatial and mathematical), Classification, Visual, Logical, and Reasoning questions.
  • Language (English/Regional): Comprehension, Grammar, Sentence Formation, Antonyms, Synonyms, and Vocabulary.
  • General Knowledge: Science, Social Studies (History, Geography, Civics), and Current Affairs.

For Class IX:

  • Mathematics: Topics from Class VIII CBSE syllabus, including Rational Numbers, Exponents, Squares, Cubes, Algebra, Mensuration, Data Handling, and Geometry.
  • Intelligence: Advanced Analogies, Patterns, Classification, and Logical/Verbal/Non-Verbal Reasoning.
  • English: Advanced Comprehension, Grammar, Vocabulary, and Sentence Structure.
  • General Science: Physics, Chemistry, and Biology topics from the Class VIII curriculum.
  • Social Science: History, Geography, and Civics/Polity topics from the Class VIII curriculum.

⚙️Selection Process

The selection process consists of three main stages:

  1. Step 1: Written Examination (AISSEE). Candidates are shortlisted based on their performance in this OMR-based test.
  2. Step 2: Medical Examination. Shortlisted candidates undergo a thorough medical fitness test at designated military hospitals.
  3. Step 3: Final Merit List. The final merit list is prepared based on the marks in the written exam, medical fitness, reservation criteria, and school-wise vacancies.

🌟Benefits of Studying in a Sainik School

  • Provides a disciplined, military-style environment that fosters leadership and patriotism.
  • Serves as the primary feeder institution for the National Defence Academy (NDA).
  • Focuses on holistic development, including academics, sports, and co-curricular activities.
  • Offers high-quality education (CBSE curriculum) at a subsidized cost.
  • Builds character, physical fitness, and mental toughness.

🏃‍♂️School Life & Curriculum

Life at a Sainik School is highly structured, blending academic rigor with physical training. The curriculum follows the CBSE pattern, but the daily routine includes physical training (PT), drills, sports, and other activities designed to prepare cadets for a career in the armed forces.

🎁Facilities in Sainik Schools

  • Well-equipped classrooms and smart classes.
  • Hostel accommodation and mess facilities.
  • Extensive sports grounds, swimming pools, and gymnasiums.
  • Obstacle courses and training areas for military-oriented activities.
  • Well-stocked libraries and computer labs.

🎯Scope

The primary scope for a Sainik School cadet is to join the Indian Armed Forces through the National Defence Academy (NDA) and other military academies. However, the excellent all-round education also prepares them for successful careers in civil services, engineering, medicine, and other fields.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The AISSEE exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically in the first or second week of January.

📝How to Apply

The application process is managed by the National Testing Agency (NTA). Candidates must apply online through the official AISSEE NTA website. The process involves registration, filling the application form, uploading a photograph/signature, and paying the fee online.

💸Fees

The application fee is set by the NTA. It is typically around ₹650 for General/OBC/Defence category candidates and ₹500 for SC/ST category candidates. This fee is subject to change.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

  • For Class VI: 150 minutes (2.5 hours).
  • For Class IX: 180 minutes (3 hours).
