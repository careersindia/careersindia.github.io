UCEED Exam Details (Undergraduate Common Entrance Exam for Design)

Eligibility
Candidates must have passed the Class 12 (or equivalent) examination in any stream (Science, Commerce, or Arts). An age limit also applies.
Scope
Admission to the Bachelor of Design (B.Des) programs at IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad, IIITDM Jabalpur, and other result-sharing institutes.
How to Apply
Online registration via the official UCEED website hosted by IIT Bombay.
Exam Structure
A 3-hour exam with two parts. Part-A is a Computer-Based Test (NAT, MSQ, MCQ). Part-B is an offline drawing and sketching test.
Score Pattern
Part-A has varied marking, with negative marking for MCQs only. Part-B is evaluated for drawing skills. Final merit is based on the total score.
Fees
Approximately ₹3,800 for General/OBC Male candidates; ₹1,900 for SC/ST/PwD and all Female candidates.
Exam Timing
Conducted once a year, typically on a Sunday in the third or fourth week of January.
Results
Announced on the official UCEED website, usually in the second week of March.
