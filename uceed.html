DETAILS ABOUT UCEED EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Undergraduate Common Entrance Examination for Design (UCEED).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: A candidate should have passed the Class XII (or equivalent) examination in **any stream** (Science, Commerce, or Arts/Humanities).
  • Year of Appearance: Candidates who have appeared for the Class XII examination for the first time in the preceding or current year are eligible.
  • Number of Attempts: A candidate can attempt UCEED a maximum of two times in two consecutive years.

🎂Age Limit

Yes, there is an age limit. The candidate should have been born on or after October 1, 1999 (age relaxation of 5 years applies for SC, ST, and PwD categories).

🎨Detailed Syllabus

The exam has two parts, testing a candidate's aptitude for design.

  • Part A (Objective): Visualization and Spatial Ability, Observation and Design Sensitivity, Environmental and Social Awareness, Analytical and Logical Reasoning, Language and Creativity, Design Thinking and Problem Solving.
  • Part B (Subjective): This part consists of one question to test the candidate's drawing and design aptitude. It requires sketching skills to communicate ideas effectively.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Appear for UCEED Exam. All candidates must appear for both Part A and Part B.
  2. Step 2: Merit Rank Calculation. A merit rank is calculated based on the combined performance in Part A and Part B. A minimum qualifying score in each part is required.
  3. Step 3: Joint Admission Process (Counselling). Candidates who secure a merit rank participate in a centralized online counselling process for seat allotment to the Bachelor of Design (B.Des) programs at participating IITs and IIITDM Jabalpur.

💰Salary Potential (After B.Des)

A Bachelor of Design (B.Des) from a top IIT is a highly prestigious degree, leading to excellent career opportunities.

  • Average Salary Package: Approximately ₹15 Lakhs to ₹22 Lakhs per annum.
  • Highest Salary Package: Can go up to ₹40 Lakhs to ₹80 Lakhs per annum for exceptional candidates in top product design and UI/UX roles.

💼Starting Post & Career Paths

Graduates begin their careers in creative and high-impact roles, including:

  • UI/UX Designer
  • Product Designer
  • Graphic Designer
  • Interaction Designer
  • Automobile/Industrial Designer
  • Animator

📋Job Profile of a Designer

A designer's job is to solve problems by creating intuitive and aesthetically pleasing user experiences, products, and visuals. This involves user research, brainstorming, sketching, creating digital prototypes, and collaborating with engineers and marketing teams to bring ideas to life.

🎁Facilities After Selection (in an IIT)

  • Access to world-class design studios, prototyping labs (3D printers, laser cutters), and workshops.
  • A curriculum that balances theoretical knowledge with hands-on, practical projects.
  • A vibrant and competitive peer group from across the country.
  • Strong industry connections for internships and placements with top design firms and tech giants.

🎯Scope

UCEED is the premier entrance exam for admission to the prestigious Bachelor of Design (B.Des) programs at IIT Bombay, IIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, IIT Hyderabad, and IIITDM Jabalpur. Many other top design colleges also use UCEED scores for admission. A UCEED qualification is a launchpad to a creative, innovative, and high-growth career in the design industry.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The UCEED exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically in the third week of January.

📝How to Apply

The application process is conducted online through the official UCEED website, which is organized by IIT Bombay. The process involves registration, filling the application form, uploading documents, and paying the fee.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Part Type Mode Max Marks Duration
Part A Objective (NAT, MSQ, MCQ) Computer-Based 240 2 Hours 30 Mins
Part B Subjective (Drawing/Sketching) Pen & Paper 60 30 Minutes

Scoring Pattern: Part A has a complex marking scheme with negative marking for some sections. Part B is evaluated subjectively. The final UCEED score is a weighted sum of marks from both parts.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 3 hours, conducted in a single session.

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared in the first week of March, approximately 5-6 weeks after the exam.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹3,600 for male candidates (General/EWS/OBC) and ₹1,800 for female candidates and SC/ST/PwD candidates.
