DETAILS ABOUT JNVST EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST).

✅Eligibility

  • For Class VI: The candidate must be studying in Class V in a government-recognized school in the same district where the JNV is located. A minimum of 75% of seats are reserved for students from rural areas.
  • For Class IX (Lateral Entry): The candidate must be studying in Class VIII in a recognized school in the district where the JNV is located.

🎂Age Limit

The age limits are very specific and are based on a defined date range in the official notification. Typically:

  • For Class VI: A candidate should be between 10 and 12 years of age.
  • For Class IX: A candidate should be between 13 and 15 years of age.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • For Class VI:
    • Mental Ability Test (MAT): Figure matching, pattern completion, analogies, and other non-verbal reasoning questions.
    • Arithmetic Test: Number system, HCF & LCM, fractions, percentage, profit & loss, simple interest.
    • Language Test: Reading comprehension, grammar, and vocabulary.
  • For Class IX:
    • English, Hindi, Mathematics, and General Science. The difficulty level is based on the Class VIII curriculum.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: JNVST Written Examination. This is the only selection test. It is a pen-and-paper OMR-based exam.
  2. Step 2: Merit List and Document Verification. A merit list is prepared based on the marks obtained in the test. Shortlisted candidates are called for document verification to confirm their eligibility (especially rural area status).
  3. Step 3: Medical Examination. A basic medical check-up is conducted.

🏫Starting Post & Department (NA)

This is an entrance exam for admission to **Class VI** or **Class IX** (via Lateral Entry) in a Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya (JNV).

📋Job Profile (NA)

NA (This is a school entrance exam).

🎁Facilities After Selection (in a JNV)

JNVs are fully residential schools run by the Government of India, offering a wide range of free facilities:

  • Free education, including board, lodging, uniforms, and textbooks.
  • High-quality education following the CBSE curriculum.
  • A three-language formula to promote national integration.
  • Excellent sports and co-curricular facilities.
  • A migration scheme where students spend one year at a JNV in a different linguistic region.

🎯Scope

JNVST is a life-changing opportunity for talented students, especially from rural backgrounds, to receive a high-quality modern education completely free of cost. JNVs are known for their excellent academic results and for preparing students for top competitive exams like JEE and NEET, providing a strong foundation for a successful career.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The JNVST is conducted once a year for each class.
  • Exam Months: The exam for Class VI is typically held in two phases: January (for winter-bound regions) and April (for summer-bound regions). The Class IX exam is usually held in February.

📝How to Apply

The application process is conducted online through the official Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) admission portal. It is completely free of cost.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

  • For Class VI: 120 minutes (2 hours).
  • For Class IX: 150 minutes (2.5 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 1 to 2 months after the examination date.

💸Fees

The application for the JNVST is **completely free**. Education and accommodation at JNVs are also free of cost.
