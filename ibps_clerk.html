DETAILS ABOUT IBPS CLERK EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Institute of Banking Personnel Selection - Clerk (IBPS Clerk).

✅Eligibility

  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India (or other eligible categories as per Govt. norms).
  • Academic Qualification: A Degree (Graduation) in any discipline from a University recognised by the Govt. of India.
  • Computer Literacy: Operating and working knowledge in computer systems is mandatory.
  • Language Proficiency: Proficiency in the Official Language of the State/UT for which a candidate applies is preferable.

🎂Age Limit

The candidate must be between **20 and 28 years** of age. Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/PwD) as per government rules.

📖Detailed Syllabus

  • Reasoning Ability: Puzzles, Seating Arrangements, Syllogism, Inequality, Blood Relations, Coding-Decoding, Direction Sense.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Data Interpretation (DI), Number Series, Simplification/Approximation, Quadratic Equations, and Arithmetic problems (Percentage, Profit & Loss, etc.).
  • English Language: Reading Comprehension, Cloze Test, Error Spotting, Sentence Improvement, Para Jumbles.
  • General/Financial Awareness: Current Affairs (last 6 months), Banking & Financial Awareness, Static GK, and knowledge of the Indian financial system.

⚙️Selection Process

The selection process is conducted in two main phases (note: there is no interview for the Clerk post):

  1. Phase-I: Preliminary Examination. An online objective test that is qualifying in nature.
  2. Phase-II: Main Examination. An online objective test. Marks from this phase are used for the final merit list.
  3. Step 3: Language Proficiency Test (LPT). Candidates who are shortlisted must appear for an LPT if they haven't studied the local language of the state they applied for in their 10th or 12th standard. This is qualifying.

💰Starting Salary Potential

The starting in-hand salary for an IBPS Clerk in a metropolitan city, including all allowances, is approximately ₹35,000 - ₹45,000 per month.

🏦Starting Post & Department

The starting post is **Clerk** (also known as Single Window Operator or Customer Service Associate) in one of the participating Public Sector Banks (PSBs) like Bank of Baroda, Canara Bank, Punjab National Bank, etc.

📋Job Profile

A Bank Clerk is the first point of contact for customers. The job is a frontline, customer-facing role with responsibilities including:

  • Handling cash transactions (deposits, withdrawals, etc.).
  • Opening new accounts and processing customer requests.
  • Addressing customer queries and providing information about banking products.
  • Performing back-office tasks like clearing cheques and maintaining ledgers.

🎁Facilities After Selection

  • A secure job in the public sector with a stable career path.
  • Defined working hours and a five-day work week (most banks).
  • Medical benefits for self and family, and Leave Travel Concession (LTC).
  • Access to concessional rates on loans (housing, vehicle, etc.).
  • Pension benefits under the National Pension System (NPS).

🎯Scope

An IBPS Clerk position is the entry point into the public sector banking industry. It offers excellent scope for growth. Clerks can appear for internal promotional exams (like JAIIB/CAIIB) and can be promoted to the officer cadre (Probationary Officer / Scale 1 Officer) within 3-5 years, leading to a managerial career path.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The IBPS Clerk exam is conducted annually.
  • Exam Months: The Preliminary exam is typically held in August/September, and the Main exam is held in October.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official IBPS website. The process involves registration, filling the application form, uploading documents, and paying the application fee online.
