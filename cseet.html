DETAILS ABOUT CSEET EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Company Secretary Executive Entrance Test (CSEET).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: A candidate who has passed or is appearing in the Senior Secondary (10+2) Examination or an equivalent examination is eligible to appear in the CSEET.
  • Exemptions: Graduates/Postgraduates and candidates who have passed CA/CMA Final are exempted from CSEET and can take direct admission to the CS Executive Programme.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for appearing for the CSEET.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The exam consists of four main papers conducted as a single test:

  • Paper 1: Business Communication: English grammar, vocabulary, and communication skills.
  • Paper 2: Legal Aptitude & Logical Reasoning: Indian Constitution, elements of company law, contracts, torts, and reasoning skills.
  • Paper 3: Economic and Business Environment: Basics of demand and supply, national income, Indian economy, and business environment.
  • Paper 4: Current Affairs and Presentation Skills: Current events of national and international importance.

⚙️Selection Process

CSEET is a qualifying entrance test. The process is straightforward:

  1. Step 1: Appear for the CSEET. This is a remote proctored online test.
  2. Step 2: Qualify the Exam. Candidates must secure a minimum of 40% in each paper and 50% in aggregate to pass.
  3. Step 3: Register for CS Executive. Passed candidates are then eligible to register for the next level of the Company Secretary course, the CS Executive Programme.

💰Starting Salary Potential (NA)

NA. This is an entrance test to a professional course. The salary potential is realized only after qualifying all three levels (Foundation, Executive, Final) and becoming a member of ICSI.

NA (This is an entrance test).

🎁Benefits of the CS Path

  • A prestigious professional qualification leading to a high-level corporate career.
  • Expertise in corporate laws, governance, and compliance.
  • Opportunity to become a Key Managerial Personnel (KMP) of a company.
  • High demand in the corporate sector due to increasing regulatory compliance.

🎯Scope

CSEET is the mandatory gateway for students after 10+2 to enter the Company Secretary profession. Clearing CSEET is the first step towards a rewarding career as a corporate governance professional, advising the board of directors on legal and statutory compliance, and rising to senior management positions in companies or starting an independent practice.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The CSEET is conducted **four times a year**.
  • Exam Months: Typically in January, May, July, and November.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must register online for CSEET through the official website of the Institute of Company Secretaries of India (ICSI).

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Part Subject No. of Questions Marks
1 Business Communication 35 50
2 Legal Aptitude & Logical Reasoning 35 50
3 Economic & Business Environment 35 50
4 Current Affairs 15 20
Total 120 170

Scoring Pattern: There is **no negative marking** in the CSEET exam.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the computer-based test is 120 minutes (2 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results are declared very quickly, usually within **10 days** of the examination.

💸Fees

The registration fee for CSEET is typically around ₹2,000, which includes the cost of study materials. This fee is subject to change.
