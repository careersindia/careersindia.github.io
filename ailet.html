ℹ️Full Form

All India Law Entrance Test (AILET).

✅Eligibility

  • B.A. LL.B. (Hons.): Senior Secondary School Examination (10+2 system) or an equivalent examination with a minimum of 45% aggregate marks (40% for SC/ST/Persons with Disabilities).
  • LL.M.: LL.B. or an equivalent law degree with a minimum of 50% marks (45% for SC/ST/Persons with Disabilities).
  • Ph.D.: LL.M. or an equivalent law degree with a minimum of 55% marks (50% for SC/ST/Persons with Disabilities).

🎂Age Limit

There is **no upper age limit** for any of the programs (B.A. LL.B., LL.M., or Ph.D.).

📖Detailed Syllabus (for B.A. LL.B.)

The AILET has a unique pattern that focuses heavily on comprehension and reasoning skills.

  • Section A: English Language: Reading comprehension passages to test understanding, inference, and vocabulary in context.
  • Section B: Current Affairs & General Knowledge: Questions are based on comprehension of passages related to contemporary events of significance from India and the world. Static GK is less emphasized.
  • Section C: Logical Reasoning: This is the largest section. It includes critical thinking, analytical reasoning, legal reasoning (based on principles, not prior legal knowledge), and logical puzzles.

⚙️Selection Process

The selection process is straightforward:

  1. Step 1: AILET Written Examination. This is the single, decisive stage.
  2. Step 2: Merit List and Counselling. A merit list is prepared based solely on the marks obtained in the AILET. Shortlisted candidates are then called for online counselling and seat allotment.

💰Starting Salary Potential

Graduates from National Law University (NLU), Delhi, are among the most sought-after in the country. Starting salaries vary widely based on the career path:

  • Corporate Law Firms (Top Tier): Approximately ₹15 Lakhs to ₹20 Lakhs per annum.
  • Judicial Services/Civil Services: As per government pay scales.
  • Litigation/Private Practice: Varies significantly.

⚖️Starting Post & Department

After a B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) from NLU Delhi, graduates can start as:

  • Associate at a Corporate Law Firm.
  • Legal Advisor in a company's in-house legal department.
  • Judicial Officer after clearing judicial services exams.
  • Independent Litigator after registering with the Bar Council.
  • Research Associate at a think tank or academic institution.

📋Job Profile

A law graduate's job profile is diverse, involving legal research, drafting contracts and legal documents, advising clients on legal matters, representing clients in court (litigation), and ensuring legal compliance for corporations.

🎁Facilities after Selection

As a student at NLU Delhi, one of India's premier law schools, you get access to:

  • A world-class library and digital research databases.
  • Moot court halls and legal aid clinics for practical training.
  • A highly-regarded faculty of legal scholars and practitioners.
  • Excellent placement and internship opportunities with top law firms and companies.
  • On-campus residential facilities.

🎯Scope

The scope after graduating from NLU Delhi is vast. Graduates are well-positioned for top-tier careers in corporate law, litigation, judiciary, civil services (UPSC), academia, and policy research. The NLU Delhi brand name provides a significant advantage in the job market.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The AILET exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically in December.

📝How to Apply

Applications are to be filled online only through the official website of NLU Delhi. The process involves registration, filling the application form, uploading a photograph and signature, and paying the application fee.
