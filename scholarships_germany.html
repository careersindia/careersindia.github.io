  • Note: Not applicable (multiple institutions). The scholarship is tenable at various highly-ranked German universities depending on the chosen course.

LevelPostgraduate (Master's and PhD)
Eligibility / Features
  • Academic Requirements: Must hold a four-year Bachelor's degree with results well above average.
  • Standardized Test Scores: English proficiency is required (IELTS Band 6, TOEFL 550 PBT, 213 CBT, 80 iBT). Some courses may be in German and require German language proficiency.
  • Field of Study: A specific list of postgraduate courses focused on development in fields like Engineering, Regional Planning, Agricultural Sciences, Health Sciences, and Social Sciences.
  • Other: Must have at least two years of proven, relevant professional experience. Must be a citizen of a developing country (including India).

ApplicationApply directly to the respective university offering the EPOS course. Do not apply to the DAAD directly. The university's selection committee nominates candidates to the DAAD.
Documents
  • Statement of Purpose (SOP): A detailed statement of motivation is required.
  • Letters of Recommendation (LORs): A letter of recommendation from your current employer is required.
  • Academic Transcripts: Required.
  • Proof of English proficiency (TOEFL/IELTS scores): Required.
  • CV or Resume: A hand-signed CV (Europass format) is required.
  • Passport copy: Required.
