ℹ️Full Form

Common University Entrance Test (Postgraduate).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: The candidate must hold a Bachelor's degree (or equivalent) from a recognized university.
  • Final Year Students: Candidates appearing in the final year of their Bachelor's degree are also eligible to apply.
  • University Criteria: Candidates must also satisfy any specific eligibility criteria set by the university they are applying to for their chosen PG program.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for candidates appearing for the CUET-PG.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The syllabus for CUET-PG is **subject-specific**. Each postgraduate course has a unique paper code and a corresponding syllabus, which is based on the typical undergraduate curriculum for that subject.

  • Domain-Specific Papers: For subjects like M.A. History, M.Sc. Physics, M.Com, etc., the syllabus covers topics from the respective Bachelor's degree program.
  • General Papers (e.g., for MBA, LLB): Some courses use a general paper syllabus which may include Language Comprehension, General Knowledge, Computer Basics, General Aptitude & Logical Reasoning.
  • Candidates must check the official Information Bulletin for the specific syllabus of their chosen paper code.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Appear for the CUET-PG Exam. This is a Computer-Based Test (CBT).
  2. Step 2: NTA Scorecard Release. The National Testing Agency (NTA) releases the results in the form of a scorecard.
  3. Step 3: Apply Separately to Universities. A CUET-PG score does not guarantee admission. Candidates must use their scorecard to apply individually to each participating university.
  4. Step 4: University Counselling. Each university conducts its own admission process and counselling based on its merit list, which is prepared using the CUET-PG scores.

💰Salary Potential (After PG)

NA. This depends entirely on the postgraduate degree obtained (e.g., an MBA from a top university will have a very high starting salary, while an M.A. in a social science may lead to academic or research roles with different pay scales).

🚀Starting Post & Career Paths

NA. Career paths are determined by the specific Master's degree (MA, MSc, MCom, MBA, etc.) pursued by the candidate after qualifying the exam.

NA (This is an entrance exam for postgraduate courses).

🎁Facilities in Participating Universities

  • Access to postgraduate departments in prestigious Central Universities like JNU, BHU, HCU, and Delhi University.
  • Advanced research facilities, specialized libraries, and expert faculty.
  • Opportunities for research, publications, and attending seminars.
  • A diverse and intellectually stimulating peer group.

🎯Scope

CUET-PG is a national-level entrance exam for admission to various postgraduate programs (MA, MSc, MCom, MBA, etc.) in a large number of Central, State, Deemed, and Private Universities across India. It provides a single platform for students to seek admission to PG courses, streamlining the admission process.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The CUET-PG exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically held over a window of several days in March.

📝How to Apply

The application process is managed by the NTA. Candidates must register and apply online through the official CUET-PG website. During registration, they must carefully select the test paper codes corresponding to the courses they wish to apply for.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Parameter Details
Exam Mode Computer-Based Test (CBT)
Number of Questions 75 (Multiple Choice Questions)
Maximum Marks 300

Scoring Pattern: +4 marks for every correct answer, -1 mark for every incorrect answer.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 105 minutes (1 hour and 45 minutes).

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 3 to 4 weeks after the conclusion of the exam period, usually in April.

💸Fees

The application fee depends on the number of test papers chosen. It typically starts around ₹1,200 for up to two test papers for General category candidates, with additional fees for more papers. Fees are lower for reserved categories.

🌐Official Website

For all official notifications, application forms, and authentic information, always refer to the official NTA CUET-PG website: pgcuet.samarth.ac.in
