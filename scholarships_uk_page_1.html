Chevening Scholarships

HostUK, at any UK university
Rankings / Featured In
  • Note: Not applicable (multiple institutions). The scholarship can be used at any UK university, including top-ranked institutions like the University of Oxford, University of Cambridge, and Imperial College London.
Websitehttps://www.chevening.org
LevelPostgraduate (one-year Master's degree)
Eligibility / Features
  • Academic Requirements: Must have an undergraduate degree that is equivalent to at least an upper second-class (2:1) honours degree in the UK.
  • Standardized Test Scores: Applicants must meet the English language requirement of their chosen UK university. Chevening no longer requires a separate English test score for the application itself, but it's essential for the university offer.
  • Field of Study: All fields are eligible, but applicants are encouraged to relate their chosen field of study to their future career goals and leadership potential.
  • Other: Must be a citizen of a Chevening-eligible country (including India) and agree to return to your country of citizenship for a minimum of two years after your award has ended. [1] Must have at least two years of work experience (equivalent to 2,800 hours). [1]
DeadlineThe application window is typically from early August to early November for study commencing the following September.
ValueThis is a fully funded scholarship covering university tuition fees, a monthly living allowance (stipend), travel costs to and from the UK, an arrival allowance, a homeward departure allowance, the cost of one visa application, and a travel grant to attend Chevening events in the UK. [1]
ApplicationApply directly through the Chevening online application portal. Applicants must select and apply for three eligible UK university courses alongside their Chevening application. [1] The process involves an online application, shortlisting, submission of references and education documents, and an interview in your home country.
Documents
  • Statement of Purpose (SOP): A series of four essays on leadership, networking, your proposed UK study, and your career plan.
  • Letters of Recommendation (LORs): Two letters of recommendation are required, which are submitted after being shortlisted.
  • Academic Transcripts: Required from all higher education institutions attended.
  • Proof of English proficiency (TOEFL/IELTS scores): Required to receive an unconditional offer from your chosen university.
  • CV or Resume: Details of work experience are entered into the online application form.
  • Passport copy: Required to prove citizenship.
EstablishedNot Applicable (multiple universities).

LevelPostgraduate (Master's)
Eligibility / Features
  • Academic Requirements: Must hold a Bachelor's degree of at least upper second-class (2:1) honours standard, or a second-class degree (2:2) and a relevant postgraduate qualification. [2]
  • Standardized Test Scores: Must meet the specific English language requirements of the chosen university/course.
  • Field of Study: Study must be in a field related to one of six CSC development themes: Science and technology for development, Strengthening health systems, Promoting global prosperity, Strengthening global peace, Strengthening resilience, and Access, inclusion and opportunity. [2]
  • Other: Must be a citizen or have been granted refugee status by a Commonwealth country, or be a British Protected Person. [2] Must be unable to afford to study in the UK without the scholarship. [2]
DeadlineThe application deadline to the CSC is typically in mid-December of the year prior to the study year.
ValueCovers tuition fees, a monthly stipend (£1,347, or £1,652 for those at universities in the London metropolitan area), return airfare to the UK, an arrival allowance, and grants for study-related travel. [2]
ApplicationCandidates must apply to a national nominating agency in their home country (e.g., the Ministry of Education in India) in the first instance. Applicants must also make a direct application to the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission (CSC) using the CSC’s online application system. [2]
Documents
  • Statement of Purpose (SOP): A detailed development impact statement is a key part of the application.
  • Letters of Recommendation (LORs): At least two references are required.
  • Academic Transcripts: Required.
  • Proof of English proficiency (TOEFL/IELTS scores): Required for the university offer.
  • CV or Resume: May be required by the nominating agency.
  • Passport copy: Required.
  • Proof of financial need (if applicable): A declaration of being unable to afford UK study is a core eligibility requirement.
