DETAILS ABOUT CLAT EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Common Law Admission Test (CLAT).

✅Eligibility

  • For UG-CLAT (5-Year LL.B.): 10+2 or an equivalent examination with a minimum of 45% marks (40% for SC/ST candidates).
  • For PG-CLAT (LL.M.): An LL.B. Degree or an equivalent examination with a minimum of 50% marks (45% for SC/ST candidates).
  • Candidates appearing in their qualifying examination are also eligible to apply.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no upper age limit** for appearing in either UG-CLAT or PG-CLAT.

📖Detailed Syllabus (for UG-CLAT)

The exam is comprehension-based, testing aptitude and reasoning skills rather than prior knowledge.

  • English Language: Comprehension of passages, drawing inferences, and understanding vocabulary and arguments in context.
  • Current Affairs, including General Knowledge: Reading passages derived from news and non-fiction to understand contemporary events of significance from India and the world.
  • Legal Reasoning: Reading passages with legal principles, applying these principles to factual situations. No prior legal knowledge is required.
  • Logical Reasoning: Critical reasoning, identifying arguments, premises, and conclusions, and drawing inferences.
  • Quantitative Techniques: Interpretation of data presented in graphs, charts, and text. Questions are of Class 10th level.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: CLAT Written Examination. This is a single, offline, pen-and-paper (OMR-based) test.
  2. Step 2: Merit List Declaration. A merit list is prepared based solely on the marks obtained in the CLAT exam.
  3. Step 3: Centralized Counselling. Candidates who secure a rank participate in a centralized online counselling process for seat allotment to the participating National Law Universities (NLUs).

💰Salary Potential (After 5-Year LL.B. from top NLUs)

Graduates from top NLUs (like NLSIU Bangalore, NALSAR Hyderabad) are highly sought-after.

  • Top Corporate Law Firms: Starting salaries (Day Zero placements) are approximately ₹18 Lakhs to ₹25 Lakhs per annum.
  • In-House Counsel/Companies: Can range from ₹8 Lakhs to ₹15 Lakhs per annum.
  • Judicial Services/Civil Services: As per government pay scales.

⚖️Starting Post & Career Paths

After graduating from a top NLU, typical career paths include:

  • Associate at a top-tier corporate law firm.
  • Legal Advisor in a multinational corporation's legal team.
  • Judicial Officer after clearing state judicial services exams.
  • Civil Servant (IAS, IPS, etc.) after clearing the UPSC CSE.
  • Litigator, starting a practice in courts.

🎁Facilities After Selection (in an NLU)

  • Rigorous academic environment with a focus on Socratic teaching methods.
  • World-class infrastructure including moot court halls and extensive libraries.
  • Highly active student bodies for debating, mooting, and publications.
  • Excellent placement cells with strong connections to top law firms and corporations.
  • Opportunities for international internships and exchange programs.

🎯Scope

CLAT is the primary gateway to the 24 participating National Law Universities (NLUs) across India (except NLU Delhi, which has its own AILET exam). An NLU degree is the gold standard for legal education in the country, providing a launchpad for elite careers in law, public policy, and civil services.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The CLAT exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Month: Typically in December.

📝How to Apply

Applications are to be filled online only through the official website of the Consortium of NLUs. The process involves registration, filling the application form, choosing NLU preferences, and paying the application fee.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern (for UG-CLAT)

Section Approx. No. of Questions Maximum Marks
English Language 22-26 120
Current Affairs, including GK 28-32
Legal Reasoning 28-32
Logical Reasoning 22-26
Quantitative Techniques 10-14
Total 120 120

Scoring Pattern: +1 mark for every correct answer, -0.25 marks for every incorrect answer.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration of the exam is 120 minutes (2 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 3 to 4 weeks after the examination date, usually by late December or early January.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹4,000 for General/OBC candidates and ₹3,500 for SC/ST/BPL candidates. This fee is subject to change.

🌐Official Website

For all official notifications, application forms, and authentic information, always refer to the official Consortium of NLUs website: consortiumofnlus.ac.in
