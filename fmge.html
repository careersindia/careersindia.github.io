ℹ️Full Form

Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE). It is also known as the Medical Council of India Screening Test.

✅Eligibility

  • Nationality: Must be a citizen of India or an Overseas Citizen of India (OCI).
  • Academic Qualification: Must possess a primary medical qualification (MBBS or equivalent) awarded by a medical institution outside India, which is confirmed by the concerned Indian Embassy.
  • Institution Recognition: The foreign medical institution must be recognized and listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** to appear for the FMGE exam.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The syllabus covers all subjects of the MBBS curriculum as prescribed in the Graduate Medical Education Regulations issued by the Medical Council of India (now NMC).

  • Pre-Clinical Subjects: Anatomy, Physiology, Biochemistry.
  • Para-Clinical Subjects: Pathology, Pharmacology, Microbiology, Forensic Medicine, Social & Preventive Medicine.
  • Clinical Subjects: Medicine, Surgery, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Paediatrics, Ophthalmology, Otorhinolaryngology (ENT), Psychiatry, Dermatology, and other allied subjects.

⚙️Selection Process

FMGE is a qualifying/licensing examination, not a selection exam for jobs. The process is straightforward:

  1. Step 1: Appear for the FMGE Computer-Based Test.
  2. Step 2: Qualify the Exam. Candidates must score a minimum of 150 marks out of 300 (i.e., 50%) to pass the examination.
  3. Step 3: Obtain Registration. Passed candidates are granted a provisional or permanent registration by the National Medical Commission (NMC) or a State Medical Council, which allows them to practice medicine in India.

💰Salary Potential (After Qualifying)

After clearing FMGE, candidates must complete a mandatory Compulsory Rotating Medical Internship (CRMI) in India. During this, they receive a stipend. After getting full registration, their salary is on par with Indian medical graduates.

  • Internship Stipend: Varies by state, typically ₹20,000 - ₹30,000 per month.
  • Starting Salary (as Junior Resident): Approximately ₹60,000 - ₹90,000 per month in a government hospital.

⚕️Starting Post & Career Paths (After Qualifying)

After qualifying FMGE and completing the internship, candidates are registered medical practitioners. They can:

  • Work as a Junior Resident or Medical Officer in government or private hospitals.
  • Prepare for the **NEET-PG** exam to pursue postgraduate specialization (MD/MS).
  • Start their own private practice or clinic.
  • Join the Army Medical Corps (AMC) or other government health services.

📋Job Profile

NA (This is a licensing exam. The job profile is that of a medical doctor, which involves diagnosing and treating patients).

🎁Facilities After Selection (NA)

NA (This is not an admission or recruitment exam for a specific institute).

🎯Scope

The scope of the FMGE is singular and critical: it is the **mandatory gateway for any Indian citizen with a foreign medical degree to legally practice modern medicine in India**. Qualifying this exam grants the candidate the same rights, privileges, and opportunities as a doctor who has completed their MBBS from an Indian medical college, including the eligibility to appear for postgraduate entrance exams like NEET-PG.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The FMGE is conducted twice a year.
  • Exam Months: Typically in June and December.

📝How to Apply

The application process is managed by the National Board of Examinations in Medical Sciences (NBEMS). Candidates must apply online through the official NBE website during the specified registration window.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Parameter Details
Exam Mode Computer-Based Test (CBT)
Number of Questions 300 (Multiple Choice Questions)
Maximum Marks 300

Scoring Pattern: +1 mark for every correct answer. There is **no negative marking** for incorrect answers.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The total duration is 300 minutes (5 hours). The exam is conducted in two parts of 150 minutes (2.5 hours) each, on the same day.

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 2 to 3 weeks after the examination date.

💸Fees

The examination fee is relatively high, typically around ₹7,080 (including GST). This fee is subject to change.

🌐Official Website

For all official notifications, bulletins, and application forms, always refer to the official NBE website: nbe.edu.in
