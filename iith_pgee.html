DETAILS ABOUT IITH-PGEE

ℹ️Full Form

Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad - Postgraduate Entrance Examination (IITH-PGEE).

✅Eligibility

  • Primary Route: Most admissions are through the **GATE** exam. A valid GATE score is the main criteria for M.Tech/M.Des programs.
  • IITH-PGEE Route: For candidates without a GATE score, or for certain sponsored/specific programs, IITH conducts its own written test and/or interview.
  • Academic Qualification: A B.E./B.Tech degree in a relevant engineering discipline with a good academic record (typically a minimum of 60% marks or a 6.0 CGPA).

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for applying for M.Tech/M.Des programs.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The syllabus for the written test is **department-specific**. It is generally based on the GATE syllabus for the corresponding engineering branch. Candidates must check the official IITH admissions portal for the detailed syllabus of their specific program (e.g., Computer Science, Mechanical Engineering, etc.).

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Application. Candidates apply online through the IITH M.Tech admissions portal, either with their GATE score or for the direct written test.
  2. Step 2: Shortlisting. Candidates are shortlisted based on their GATE score or their academic profile.
  3. Step 3: Written Test and/or Interview. Shortlisted candidates are called to the campus (or attend online) for a written test and/or multiple rounds of technical interviews to assess their subject knowledge and research aptitude.
  4. Step 4: Final Merit List. A final selection list is prepared based on the candidate's performance in the test/interview and their academic record.

💰Stipend & Salary Potential

During M.Tech: Admitted students (through GATE) receive a monthly stipend from the Ministry of Education, which is currently ₹12,400 per month.

After M.Tech from IITH: Placements are excellent, with graduates securing top R&D and specialized roles.

  • Average Salary Package: Approximately ₹25 Lakhs to ₹30 Lakhs per annum for M.Tech.
  • Highest Salary Package: Can exceed ₹60 Lakhs to ₹1 Crore per annum for roles in AI/ML and top tech companies.

🚀Starting Post & Department

After completing an M.Tech from IITH, graduates are hired for advanced roles, such as:

  • R&D Engineer in tech companies.
  • Senior Software Engineer / SDE-2.
  • AI/ML Engineer or Data Scientist.
  • Design Engineer in core industries like semiconductor/VLSI.

  • A strong focus on research, innovation, and entrepreneurship.
  • World-renowned faculty and a flexible, fractal academic curriculum.

🎯Scope

IITH-PGEE is a highly competitive entrance process for admission to one of India's top second-generation IITs. An M.Tech or M.Des degree from IIT Hyderabad is a prestigious qualification that opens doors to elite careers in research and development, specialized technology domains, and academia. It provides a significant boost over a standard B.Tech degree, especially for roles in cutting-edge fields like AI, VLSI, and Autonomous Systems.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The admission process is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Months: The application window typically opens in **March/April**, and the written tests/interviews are usually held in **May/June**.

📝How to Apply

Candidates must apply online through the official IITH M.Tech admissions portal when the notification is released.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

The exam structure is **not fixed** and varies by department. It is generally a combination of a written test and an interview.

Component Details
Written Test Can be objective (MCQ) or subjective, testing core subject knowledge.
Interview Technical interviews to assess fundamental concepts and problem-solving skills.

There is usually no fixed scoring pattern announced beforehand; selection is based on overall performance.

⏱️Duration of the Exam (NA)

NA (The duration of the written test varies by department, typically 1 to 2 hours).

⏳Result Time

The results or final selection lists are typically released within **2 to 3 weeks** after the interviews are concluded.

💸Fees

The application fee is typically around ₹500-600 for General/OBC candidates and ₹250-300 for reserved categories. This fee is subject to change.
