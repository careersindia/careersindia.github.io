DETAILS ABOUT CUET-UG EXAM

ℹ️Full Form

Common University Entrance Test (Undergraduate).

✅Eligibility

  • Academic Qualification: The candidate must have passed the Class 12 / equivalent examination or be appearing in the current academic year.
  • Stream/Subjects: There are no stream restrictions to appear for CUET-UG. However, candidates must fulfill the specific subject eligibility criteria of the university/course they wish to apply for.

🎂Age Limit

There is **no age limit** for the candidates.

📖Detailed Syllabus

The exam is divided into three sections:

  • Section I (Languages): Consists of Section IA (13 languages) and IB (20 languages). Tests reading comprehension (factual, literary, narrative) and vocabulary.
  • Section II (Domain Subjects): Consists of 27 domain-specific subjects. The syllabus for each subject is based on the Class 12 NCERT curriculum only.
  • Section III (General Test): Tests General Knowledge, Current Affairs, General Mental Ability, Numerical Ability, Quantitative Reasoning (up to Class 8 level), and Logical & Analytical Reasoning.

⚙️Selection Process

  1. Step 1: Appear for the CUET-UG Exam. This is a Computer-Based Test (CBT), with some subjects in hybrid (pen & paper) mode.
  2. Step 2: NTA Scorecard. The National Testing Agency (NTA) releases the results in the form of a scorecard with percentile scores for each subject.
  3. Step 3: Apply to Universities. Candidates must apply **separately** to the individual universities (e.g., Delhi University, BHU, JNU) using their CUET-UG scorecard.
  4. Step 4: University Merit List & Counselling. Each university prepares its own merit list based on the candidate's CUET scores in the required subjects and conducts its own counselling for admission.

💰Placement & Salary Potential (After Graduation)

This depends entirely on the university and the course chosen. Graduates from top colleges of Delhi University, BHU, etc., can expect:

  • Average Salary Package: Varies widely. For top commerce/economics courses, it can be ₹8 Lakhs to ₹15 Lakhs per annum. For other courses, it may be lower.
  • Highest Salary Package: Can go up to ₹30-40 Lakhs per annum for students from elite colleges like SRCC or St. Stephen's.

🎁Facilities in Participating Universities

  • Access to some of the most prestigious and historic university campuses in India (e.g., Delhi University, BHU, JNU).
  • Highly qualified faculty and a strong academic environment.
  • Extensive libraries, sports facilities, and cultural societies.
  • A vibrant and diverse student community from all over the country.

🎯Scope

CUET-UG is now the single largest entrance examination in India. It is the gateway to admission in over 250 universities, including all Central Universities, many State, Deemed, and Private universities. It provides a single-window opportunity for students to seek admission in these institutions across the country, based on a common and fair evaluation.

🗓️Exam Dates & Frequency

  • Frequency: The CUET-UG exam is conducted once a year.
  • Exam Months: Typically held over a window of several days in May and June.

📝How to Apply

The application process is managed by the NTA. Candidates must register and apply online through the official CUET-UG website. The form requires selecting universities, courses, and the specific subjects (languages, domain, general test) they wish to appear for.

📊Exam Structure & Score Pattern

Section Questions To be Attempted
Section I (Languages) 50 40
Section II (Domain) 50 40
Section III (General Test) 60 50

Scoring Pattern: +5 marks for every correct answer, -1 mark for every incorrect answer.

⏱️Duration of the Exam

The duration varies. For most subjects, it is **45 minutes**. For subjects like Mathematics, Applied Mathematics, Accountancy, Physics, Chemistry, Economics, and the General Test, the duration is **60 minutes**.

⏳Result Time

The results are typically declared within 3 to 4 weeks after the conclusion of the entire exam period.

💸Fees

The application fee depends on the number of subjects chosen. It typically starts from ₹1000 for up to 3 subjects for General category candidates, with additional fees for more subjects. Fees are lower for reserved categories.
